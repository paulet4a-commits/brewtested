## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does tamper quality really matter that much?**
A: Yes. Uneven pressure creates channeling (water finding weak spots in the puck), which causes thin, sour shots. A quality tamper delivers 30 pounds of even pressure every time, guaranteeing proper extraction. The difference between a $10 tamper and a $40 tamper is the consistency of that pressure.

**Q: What's the correct tamping pressure?**
A: Professional espresso machines use 30 pounds of downward pressure. This compacts the coffee puck to the density needed for proper water saturation and 25-30 second extraction. Too light and the shot runs thin; too heavy and it chokes.

**Q: Should I use a pressure-indicator tamper or a weighted one?**
A: Pressure-indicator tampers (like Breville) are better for beginners because they remove guesswork. Weighted tampers (like Normcore) are better once you've developed feel and muscle memory. Most espresso pros eventually prefer weighted because they trust their technique.

**Q: What's the difference between a 58.5mm and 57mm tamper?**
A: These sizes correspond to portafilter basket sizes. Most espresso machines use 58.5mm baskets, but older machines or certain commercial grinders may require 57mm. Measure your basket or check your machine's manual.

**Q: Can I use a cheaper tamper from a budget starter kit?**
A: Budget tampers often have flat bases that aren't actually flat, or uneven weight distribution that creates pressure inconsistency. You'll get occasional good shots mixed with regularly sour ones. Upgrading to even a $25-30 quality tamper dramatically improves consistency.

**Q: Do I need to buy a tamper designed specifically for my machine?**
A: No. Universal 58.5mm tampers work across all standard espresso machines. Only if you have an older machine or non-standard basket should you verify basket size before purchasing.
