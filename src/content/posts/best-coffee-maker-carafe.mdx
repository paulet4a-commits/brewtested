## Buying Guide

**Thermal vs. glass carafe:** Thermal keeps coffee hot 4+ hours without a hot plate. Glass carafes cool faster but show coffee level clearly. Thermal wins for households where coffee sits 30+ minutes.

**Cup sizing:** One "cup" = ~5oz. A standard mug is 1.5–2 cups. A "12-cup" maker produces about 6–8 standard mugs.

**Temperature matters:** SCAA range (195–205°F) noticeably improves specialty bean flavor. Budget makers often brew at 185–190°F — adequate for dark roasts, limiting for light roasts.
