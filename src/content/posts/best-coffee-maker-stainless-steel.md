## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why choose stainless steel over glass carafes?**

Stainless steel thermal carafes maintain coffee temperature without a heating plate, preventing the bitter, scorched taste that comes from prolonged heat exposure. Plus, they're virtually unbreakable, making them ideal for busy kitchens, offices, and households with kids or pets.

**Q: How long does a stainless steel coffee maker typically last?**

Quality stainless steel machines like the Cuisinart and Bonavita typically last 5-7 years with proper maintenance (regular descaling, gentle carafe handling). Budget models may need replacement every 3-4 years, but the durability of stainless construction still outlasts most plastic-heavy alternatives.

**Q: Are stainless steel carafes dishwasher safe?**

Most are top-rack dishwasher safe, though hand-washing extends the lifespan of seals and finish. We recommend hand-washing thermal carafes with warm soapy water and drying immediately to maintain shine and prevent mineral buildup.

**Q: What's the difference between a thermal carafe and a regular carafe with heating plate?**

Thermal carafes (double-walled stainless steel) maintain temperature through insulation without electricity. Heating plates keep coffee warm through direct heat, but prolonged contact degrades flavor and can scorch the bottom. Thermal carafes are superior for taste and energy efficiency.
