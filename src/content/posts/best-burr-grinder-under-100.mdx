## What Makes a Burr Grinder Worth Buying?

**Burr vs blade:** Blade grinders chop unevenly — powder and chunks in the same batch. Burr grinders crush beans between two surfaces, producing uniform particle size. Uniform grind = even extraction = better coffee.

**Grind size by method:**

| Brew method | Grind size needed |
|-------------|------------------|
| French press | Coarse |
| Drip / Pour over | Medium |
| AeroPress | Medium-fine |
| Espresso | Extra fine (needs $200+ grinder) |

**Manual vs electric:** Manual gives better quality per dollar. Electric is faster and more convenient. Under $100, manual wins on cup quality.
