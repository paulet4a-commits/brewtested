## Electric With Cup Controls: Cuisinart DBM-8P1

The [Cuisinart product page](https://www.cuisinart.com/supreme-grind-automatic-burr-mill/P-DBM-8.html) identifies its listing as **DBM-8P1**. It specifies 18 grind positions, a 4–18 cup slide dial, an 8-ounce removable hopper, and an electric timer with automatic shutoff.

**Consider it if:** You want electric operation and prefer a cup-based slider over turning a seconds dial.

**Before buying:** Check the model suffix and whether the cup range fits your normal batch. A cup slider does not weigh coffee. For repeatable recipes, weigh the resulting grounds and adjust rather than assuming a selected number equals a fixed mass.

The source does not specify burr geometry or diameter. Check those details if they matter to your purchase; we have no precision-espresso or comparative noise measurements for this model.

## Choose Controls for Your Brewer

For drip, pour-over, or immersion, start with the grinder's and brewer's instructions, record setting and dose, and change one variable at a time. Beans, recipe, and brew method matter; a universal AeroPress or pour-over setting would be misleading.

For espresso, do not infer suitability from the word “fine,” burr material, or a price threshold. Check whether the exact grinder offers the adjustment needed for your basket and recipe. This comparison contains no measured espresso dialing-in results and sets no arbitrary minimum price for an espresso grinder.

Compare delivered price, exact variant, return policy, instructions, and replacement-part support before checkout. An offer qualifies only when its actual total fits your ceiling.
