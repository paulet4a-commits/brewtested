Iced coffee and cold brew are different drinks with different makers. Cold brew steeps at room temperature for 12–24 hours — smoother, less acidic, naturally sweet. Iced coffee brews hot and pours over ice — faster but more acidic.

## Cold Brew vs. Iced Coffee

| | Cold Brew | Iced Coffee |
|---|---|---|
| Brew time | 12–24 hours | 5–10 minutes |
| Acidity | Low — smooth, naturally sweet | Higher — standard coffee acidity |
| Caffeine | Higher per oz (concentrate) | Standard |
| Cost per cup | ~$0.20–0.40 | ~$0.10–0.20 |

Cold brew wins on smoothness and acidity. Iced coffee wins on speed and simplicity. Most regular iced coffee drinkers prefer cold brew once they try it.
