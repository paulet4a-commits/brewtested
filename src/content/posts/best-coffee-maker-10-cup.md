## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between thermal and glass carafes?**
A: Thermal carafes (stainless-steel insulated) keep coffee hot 60+ minutes without heating plates that burn flavor. Glass carafes sit on heating plates that extend temperature but gradually scorch oils. Choose thermal if you drink slowly, glass if you drink fresh.

**Q: How many filters should I use?**
A: Use exactly one filter per brew cycle (either paper or permanent metal). Extra filters restrict water flow and cause under-extraction. Metal filters let fine particles through for bolder flavor; paper filters create cleaner cups.

**Q: Can I use pre-ground coffee?**
A: Yes, but taste quality drops 30-40% within 15 minutes of grinding. For best results, grind immediately before brewing. If pre-ground is necessary, buy fresh weekly rather than monthly.

**Q: How often should I descale?**
A: Descale every 60-80 brews (roughly monthly with daily 10-cup use). Signs: slower brewing, weak coffee, or mineral scale visible in carafe. Most machines have a descale cycle button or run white vinegar through manually.

**Q: Should I brew smaller amounts in a 10-cup maker?**
A: For consistent quality, brew at least 5 cups. Smaller amounts change water-to-grounds contact time and heating efficiency, creating weaker or over-extracted coffee.

**Q: What's the best water type?**
A: Filtered water tastes better than tap or distilled. Filtered removes chlorine and particulates without over-softening like distilled water does. If hard water is unavoidable, descale every 40 brews instead of 80.
