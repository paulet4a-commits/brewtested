## Pour-Over Ratio Calculator: Quick Reference

**Standard 1:16 Ratio (Balanced):**
- 20g coffee → 320g water
- 25g coffee → 400g water
- 30g coffee → 480g water

**Lighter 1:15 Ratio (More flavor):**
- 20g coffee → 300g water
- 25g coffee → 375g water
- 30g coffee → 450g water

**Stronger 1:17 Ratio (More body):**
- 20g coffee → 340g water
- 25g coffee → 425g water
- 30g coffee → 510g water

**Pro tip:** Start with 1:16, then adjust up or down based on taste. Too bitter? Lower ratio (more water). Too weak? Higher ratio (less water).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I really need a scale for pour-over?**
A: Yes. Volume measurements (scoops) are inconsistent because coffee density varies. A scale eliminates guessing and ensures repeatable results. This is the single biggest improvement home brewers make.

**Q: What's the best pour-over ratio for beginners?**
A: Start with 1:16 (coffee:water). It's balanced and forgiving. Once you're comfortable, experiment with 1:15 for brighter cups or 1:17 for fuller body.

**Q: Can I use my kitchen scale instead of a coffee scale?**
A: Kitchen scales typically only measure to ±5g, which is too imprecise for coffee (a pour-over uses 20-30g coffee total). Coffee scales measure to ±0.1g for accuracy.

**Q: How long does a pour-over take with the right ratio?**
A: 3-4 minutes total. Bloom for 30 seconds with 2x the coffee weight in water, then pour the rest over 2-3 minutes.
