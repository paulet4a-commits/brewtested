## Manual vs. Electric for AeroPress

**Manual** (C3 Pro, Skerton Pro): 1–2 cups daily, travel, enjoy the ritual. 2–3 min per cup.

**Electric** (Encore, Opus, OXO): Multiple cups daily, prioritize speed, want hands-off convenience.

**Rule**: Grind consistency matters more than type. A $45 manual burr grinder beats any blade grinder.

## AeroPress Grind Setting Guide

- Too coarse (12+): watery/sour
- Too fine (1–3): bitter, over-extracted
- Sweet spot (6–8): balanced, full-bodied

Adjust one click at a time, taste between shots.
