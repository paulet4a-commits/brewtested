## FAQ

**French press vs. pour over — which makes better coffee?**
Different profiles. French press: rich, full-bodied, more oils. Pour over: clean, bright, more clarity. Neither is objectively better — depends on preference.

**Why does my French press coffee taste bitter?**
Over-extraction: grind too fine or steeped too long. Use coarse grind (sea salt texture), 4-minute steep, pour immediately.

**Do I need an expensive grinder for French press?**
