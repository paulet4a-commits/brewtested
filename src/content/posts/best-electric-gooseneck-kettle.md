## Buying Guide: Do You Need Variable Temperature?

This is the core question. Owner research shows:

**You need variable temperature if:**
- You brew multiple methods (espresso prep at 200°F, white tea at 160°F, pour-over at 195°F)
- You experiment with water temperature to control extraction
- You brew different coffee origins that need different temps (light roasts hotter, dark roasts cooler)

**Fixed 195°F is fine if:**
- You brew one main method consistently (typically pour-over)
- You don't adjust temperature based on coffee type
- Simplicity appeals to you

## Pour Precision: What Actually Matters

All five kettles have narrow goosenecks that give control. Owner tests show:

**High precision (Fellow, Breville, OXO):** Thin spout ~3mm, excellent control for delicate pours
**Medium precision (Bonavita):** Similar spout size, equally good
**Basic precision (Cosori):** Slightly wider spout, still good control, less finesse

For most pour-over brewing, the difference is minimal. The spout matters far more than brand.

## Cordless vs. Corded: Practicality Test

**Cordless (Breville, OXO, Cosori):**

- Con: Base takes permanent counter space

**Corded (Fellow, Bonavita):**

- Con: Tethered to outlet location

Owner feedback slightly favors cordless for daily convenience, but it's personal preference.

## FAQ

**Q: Is variable temperature really necessary for coffee?**
A: For standard pour-over at 195°F, no. But if you explore different coffee origins, light roasts, or other brewing methods (white tea, espresso prep water), yes—temperature dramatically affects taste.

**Q: Can I use a regular kettle instead?**
A: Regular kettles boil to 212°F and don't pour precisely. You'll burn coffee and struggle with control. A gooseneck is worth the investment if you pour regularly.

**Q: How long does it take to heat water?**
A: 3-6 minutes depending on model. Breville and Cosori are fastest (~3-4 min). Fellow and Bonavita are slowest (~5-6 min). Once at temperature, none reheat under 2 minutes.

**Q: Do I need to descale regularly?**
A: Yes. Hard water users should descale monthly; soft water users quarterly. Use white vinegar (no fancy products needed). It takes 20 minutes.

**Q: What size should I get?**
A: 0.8-1.0L is standard. For solo brewing, 0.8L (Fellow) is enough. For couples or entertaining, 1.0L (Bonavita, Breville, OXO) gives flexibility.

**Q: Does brand matter, or are they all the same?**
A: Fellow and Breville are precision-engineered with tighter tolerances. Budget models (Cosori, Bonavita) are more utilitarian but still reliable. Brand consistency matters if you brew daily.

**Q: Can I use it for other hot beverages?**
A: Yes—tea, instant hot cocoa, pasta water heating, sous-vide prep, instant noodles. Any task needing controlled-temperature water. This versatility justifies the cost for many owners.
