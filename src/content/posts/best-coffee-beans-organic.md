## FAQ: Organic Coffee Beans

**What does "organic" actually mean for coffee?**
Organic coffee is grown without synthetic pesticides, herbicides, or fertilizers. It's certified by organizations like the USDA or EU Organic authority. This matters because conventional coffee farming uses significant pesticide loads—organic farming protects the environment and potentially your health.

**Does organic coffee taste different?**
Not necessarily better, but potentially cleaner. Organic beans often taste fresher because farmers prioritize soil health, which can enhance flavor development. However, roast level and origin matter more than organic certification alone.

**How should I store organic coffee beans?**
Keep whole beans in an airtight container away from light and heat. A pantry cabinet works perfectly. Avoid the freezer (condensation damages flavor). Once opened, consume within 2 weeks for optimal freshness.

**Is fair-trade certification important?**
Fair-trade ensures coffee farmers receive a minimum price for their crop, supporting sustainable farming communities. It's a good indicator of ethical sourcing alongside organic certification. We prefer brands offering both.
