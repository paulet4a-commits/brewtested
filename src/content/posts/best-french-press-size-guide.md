## French Press Size FAQ

**What's the most popular French press size?**
The 8-cup (34oz) Bodum Chambord is the bestseller across North America. It fits households of two to four people and doesn't require constant re-brewing for heavy drinkers.

**Can I use a 12-cup press for one person?**
Yes, but you'll waste coffee and energy. A 12-cup press takes longer to preheat and cools faster with half-empty volume. Consider a 3-cup or 8-cup instead.

**How much coffee should I use per size?**
Follow the 1:15 coffee-to-water ratio: 8-cup press = 30-35g coffee, 12-cup = 50-55g coffee. Always use a kitchen scale for consistency.

**Is bigger always better?**
No. Bigger requires more counter space, heavier lifting when full, longer preheating, and faster cooling. Match your household's actual consumption pattern.
