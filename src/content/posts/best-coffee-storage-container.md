## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long do coffee beans stay fresh in a storage container?**
A: In a quality airtight container, whole beans maintain peak flavor for 3-4 weeks. Ground coffee degrades faster (1-2 weeks). Temperature and light exposure significantly impact longevity.

**Q: Can I store coffee in the freezer?**
A: Freezer storage works if you use an airtight, moisture-proof container. However, repeated thawing causes condensation that damages beans. Freeze only if you'll use the entire container without opening it.

**Q: Do coffee storage containers need a one-way valve?**
A: Not required, but helpful. Freshly roasted beans release CO2 for 1-2 weeks. A one-way valve lets gas escape without letting air in. After beans cure (2+ weeks old), a standard airtight seal works fine.

**Q: Should I store coffee beans whole or ground?**
A: Always store whole beans if possible. Grinding increases surface area, causing faster oxidation and flavor loss. Grind immediately before brewing for best results.
