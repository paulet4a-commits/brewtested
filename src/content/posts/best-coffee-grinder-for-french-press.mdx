## Why Coarse Grind Matters for French Press

French press is full immersion — grounds steep in hot water for 4 minutes. Coarse grounds:
- Prevent over-extraction during long steep
- Stay above the metal filter (no sediment in cup)
- Let you taste the bean's origin, not bitterness

**Ideal texture:** Sea salt — chunky enough to not slip through the filter, fine enough to extract flavor.

**Dial-in process:** Start coarse (settings 28–32 on Encore). Adjust one click at a time. Bitter = go coarser. Weak/sour = go finer.
