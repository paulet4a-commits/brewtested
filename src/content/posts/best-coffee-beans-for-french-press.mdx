## Why French Press Needs Coarse Grind

Four minutes of contact time means fine grounds over-extract fast — producing bitter, muddy cups. Always use the coarsest setting on your grinder. Pre-ground coffee (typically medium-fine for drip) will ruin a French press.

Buy whole beans, grind fresh, grind coarse.
