## How We Tested These Services

Our evaluation spanned three months of actual subscriptions, measuring:

1. **Roast Quality** – Tasted each coffee brewed with consistent methods (pour-over, French press) and equipment
2. **Freshness** – Measured roast date freshness on arrival and tracked flavor degradation over 4 weeks
3. **Consistency** – Compared multiple shipments from the same service for reliability
4. **Curation Quality** – Evaluated whether chosen/recommended roasts matched preferences and advertised profiles
5. **Customer Experience** – Assessed website usability, shipping speed, pause/skip ease, and support responsiveness
6. **Value** – Calculated cost-per-ounce and compared to retail specialty coffee prices

All coffees were brewed with filtered water at 195-205°F using the same grinder and brewing equipment for fair comparison.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How often should I expect deliveries?
**A:** Most services default to monthly, but all five offer flexible scheduling. You can typically receive every 2, 3, or 4 weeks, or pause your subscription entirely.

### Q: What if I don't like a roast I receive?
**A:** All services offer satisfaction guarantees. Trade Coffee and Mistobox explicitly promise to replace any roast you don't like. Contact their customer service with photos/description.

### Q: How long do beans stay fresh after arrival?
**A:** Whole beans stay fresh for 3-4 weeks after roast date in an airtight container at room temperature. All services ship beans roasted within days of shipment to maximize freshness.

### Q: Can I cancel anytime without penalty?
**A:** Yes. All five services allow cancellation, pause, or skip with no penalty. You're never locked into a contract.

### Q: What's the difference between subscription prices?
**A:** Pricing varies because: (1) bag sizes differ (12 oz vs. 16 oz), (2) roaster overhead differs (premium roasters = higher prices), (3) curation complexity differs (algorithmic curation costs less than premium hand-picking).
