## How to Make Cold Brew

1. **Grind:** Coarse — like coarse sea salt. 1 cup (85g) coffee per 4 cups (1L) water for concentrate.
2. **Combine:** Add grounds and cold filtered water. Stir to saturate all grounds.
3. **Steep:** Refrigerate 12–24 hours. 12 hours = lighter, brighter. 24 hours = richer, stronger.
4. **Filter:** Strain through your brewer's filter.
5. **Store:** Refrigerate concentrate up to 2 weeks.
6. **Serve:** Dilute concentrate 1:4 with water or milk over ice.

## Cold Brew FAQ

**How is cold brew different from iced coffee?**
Iced coffee is hot-brewed coffee poured over ice — acidic and can taste watered down. Cold brew steeps in cold water 12–24 hours, producing concentrate that's 60–70% less acidic and naturally sweeter.

**What coffee should I use?**
Medium to dark roast — chocolate, caramel, and nutty notes shine in cold brew. Light roasts can taste sour or flat. Always use coarsely ground beans.

**How long does it keep?**
Concentrate: up to 2 weeks refrigerated. Ready-to-drink: 1 week. Always store in an airtight container.
