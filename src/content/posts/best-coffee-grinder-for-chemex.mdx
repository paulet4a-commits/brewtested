## Chemex Grind Guide

**Target:** Medium-coarse — similar to sea salt.

**Extraction time:** 4–5 minutes total brew (including pours).

**If bitter:** Go coarser. **If weak/watery:** Go finer.

**Starting points:**
- Baratza Encore: settings 18–22
- Fellow Ode: settings 25–30
- Breville: settings 30–35
