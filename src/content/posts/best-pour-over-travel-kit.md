## Frequently Asked Questions

### What's the lightest pour over travel kit?

The Hario V60 Travel Kit and Nanopresso are both around 4.2 ounces, making them the lightest options. For absolute minimalism, consider the plastic Melitta brewer, which is slightly lighter and more compact. Combined with a lightweight camping kettle, you've got a complete brewing system under 8 ounces.

### Do I need to bring a kettle when traveling?

Most pour over kits (except the GSI Java Drip) require a separate way to heat water. Options include: a lightweight camping kettle, a collapsible silicone kettle, boiling water in a pot over a campfire, or using hot water from a hostel or café. For car camping or RV travel, a standard kettle is convenient.

### How fine should I grind coffee for pour over travel brewing?

Pour over coffee works best with a medium-fine grind—similar to the consistency of granulated sugar. If you're buying pre-ground coffee, most brands offer a "pour over" or "drip coffee" grind that works perfectly. For long trips, bring a small hand grinder; several ultralight options weigh under 5 ounces.

### Can I make coffee for multiple people with these kits?

The Hario V60 and Melitta brew single cups, though you can brew multiple cups in sequence. The GSI Outdoors Java Drip makes larger batches (up to 3-4 cups), making it better for groups. The Nanopresso makes espresso shots that can be combined with water for multiple servings.

### What water temperature is ideal for pour over travel coffee?

Ideal water temperature is between 195-205°F (90-96°C). If you don't have a thermometer, let boiling water cool for 30 seconds before pouring. This temperature produces better flavor extraction than boiling water while avoiding bitterness.

### Are these kits worth it, or should I just buy instant coffee?

Pour over kits produce genuinely excellent coffee that tastes dramatically better than instant. The extra 5 minutes of brewing time is worthwhile, and the ritual becomes part of the camping or travel experience. That said, instant coffee is fine for ultrafast morning brew on challenging days.
