## FAQ

**Q: What grind size is best for cold brew?**
A: Coarse—like sea salt. Finer grinds over-extract, creating bitter concentrate. Coarser grinds maximize surface area without bitterness.

**Q: How long should I steep cold brew?**
A: 12 hours is standard. 8-10 hours minimum for lighter extracts. Don't exceed 24 hours (bitter compounds emerge). Room temperature or cold—both work identically.

**Q: How much concentrate to water?**
A: Start 1:1 (equal parts concentrate + water). Too strong? Dilute 1:2. Prefer cold milk? Use 1:1 and add cold milk instead of water.

**Q: How long does cold brew last?**
A: Concentrate: 7-10 days refrigerated. Diluted drink: consume within 24 hours. Pre-made RTD: 5-7 days after opening.

**Q: Can I use hot brew coffee for cold brewing?**
A: No. Cold water extraction is fundamentally different—it releases different flavor compounds and less acid. Use coffee specifically suited to cold immersion.

## Cold Brew Brewing Guide (30 seconds)

1. **Grind coarse** (if whole bean)
2. **Ratio:** 1 part coffee : 4 parts cold water
3. **Steep:** 12 hours, room temperature
4. **Strain:** Through fine mesh or cheesecloth
5. **Dilute:** 1:1 with water or milk
6. **Serve:** Over ice
