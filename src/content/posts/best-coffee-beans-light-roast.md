## FAQ

**What's the difference between light roast and medium roast?**
Light roasts are removed from the heat just after the "first crack," preserving more of the bean's origin flavors and acidity. Medium roasts continue a bit longer, developing more body and sweetness while retaining some brightness.

**How should I store light roast coffee beans?**
Store in an airtight container away from heat, light, and moisture. Whole beans stay fresh for 2-4 weeks; ground coffee for 1-2 weeks. Avoid the freezer, as temperature fluctuations introduce moisture.

**What brewing method brings out light roast coffee best?**
Pour-over, AeroPress, and Chemex brewing methods showcase light roasts beautifully by controlling water temperature and contact time. Espresso also works well if your machine can handle lighter roasts consistently.

**Are light roasts less caffeinated than dark roasts?**
Essentially no. The myth persists, but roasting level has minimal impact on caffeine content. Light roasts may taste "brighter" and feel more caffeinated due to higher acidity and cleaner flavor.
