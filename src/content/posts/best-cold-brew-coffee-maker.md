## How to Make Cold Brew (Universal Method)

1. **Grind:** Coarse grind, like coarse sea salt. Use 1 cup (85g) coffee per 4 cups (1L) water for concentrate.
2. **Combine:** Add grounds and cold filtered water. Stir to saturate all grounds.
3. **Steep:** Refrigerate 12-24 hours. 12 hours = lighter, brighter. 24 hours = richer, stronger.
4. **Filter:** Strain through your brewer's filter (plus a paper filter for extra clarity if desired).
5. **Store:** Refrigerate concentrate up to 2 weeks.
6. **Serve:** Dilute concentrate 1:4 with water or milk over ice.

## Cold Brew FAQ

**How is cold brew different from iced coffee?**
Iced coffee is hot-brewed coffee poured over ice — acidic and can taste watered down. Cold brew steeps in cold water for 12-24 hours, producing a concentrate that's 60-70% less acidic and naturally sweeter.

**How long does cold brew last in the fridge?**
Concentrate: up to 2 weeks refrigerated. Ready-to-drink: 1 week. Always store in an airtight container.

**What coffee should I use for cold brew?**
Medium to dark roast works best — chocolate, caramel, and nutty notes shine in cold brew. Light roasts can taste sour or flat. Use coarsely ground beans for best results.

**How much coffee do I need?**
For concentrate: 1 cup grounds per 4 cups water. For ready-to-drink: 1 cup grounds per 8 cups water.

**Is cold brew stronger than regular coffee?**
Cold brew concentrate is much stronger — 2-3x the caffeine per ounce. But when diluted 1:4, the caffeine content is similar to regular coffee.

**Can I heat cold brew concentrate?**
Yes — add hot water instead of cold for a low-acid hot coffee. Some people prefer this for sensitive stomachs.
