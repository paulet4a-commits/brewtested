## Manual vs. Electric: When Manual Wins

- **Noise**: manual is silent — critical for apartments and early mornings
- **Travel**: no power needed, pocket-sized options
- **Espresso precision**: 60+ micro-adjustments rival $300 electric grinders
- **Cost**: $60 manual often beats $100 electric for grind quality

**When electric wins**: multiple cups daily, high volume, prefer speed over ritual.
