## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do puck screens actually improve espresso quality?**

Yes, measurably. Scientific testing shows 4-8% extraction improvement (quantified by TDS meter) when using quality puck screens. Blind tasting confirms that shots taste better—more complete flavor development, reduced bitterness, improved body. For casual drinkers, the improvement is noticeable but subtle. For enthusiasts pulling 5+ shots weekly, the difference becomes obvious and consistent.

**Are all 58mm puck screens compatible with my machine?**

Nearly all standard espresso machines use 58mm portafilters, making universal screens compatible. However, verify before purchasing. Some lever machines (Flair, ROK) and certain newer automated systems use different sizes. Measure your portafilter's outer basket diameter to confirm. Most machines are 58mm, but 51mm (some super-compact machines) and 57mm (rare) options exist.

**How often should I clean or replace a puck screen?**

Clean after every use by removing the screen and rinsing under hot water, then drying immediately. Weekly backflushing with the screen installed prevents trapped fine particles. Replacement depends on usage: casual users (2-3 shots weekly) should replace annually; enthusiasts (5+ daily) replace every 18-24 months. Premium titanium-coated screens last 3-5 years under daily use.

**Can I use a puck screen with a bottomless portafilter?**

No. Bottomless portafilters require unobstructed water flow from the group head directly to the basket. Inserting a screen between the shower screen and basket would block visibility. Standard portafilters with shower screens accommodate puck screens perfectly.

**Do puck screens increase brewing time or resistance?**

Minimally. Quality screens (IMS, Normcore) add 1-2 seconds to total extraction time and produce imperceptible resistance increase. You may need to adjust grind slightly coarser (one step on most grinders) to maintain your target 25-30 second pull time. This grind adjustment actually improves extraction because coarser grounds with even water distribution extract better than fine grounds with channeling.

**What's the difference between precision-engineered and basic stainless puck screens?**

Precision-engineered screens (IMS, Espresso Gear) maintain hole tolerances within 0.1mm, ensuring consistent water distribution across the coffee bed. Basic screens may have variations within 0.3-0.5mm, reducing distribution consistency slightly. At home use volumes, basic quality screens perform admirably. For high-volume use or competition contexts, precision engineering matters more.
