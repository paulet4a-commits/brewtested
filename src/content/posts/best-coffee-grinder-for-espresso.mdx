## Why Your Grinder Matters More Than Your Machine

A $100 machine with a $300 grinder beats a $1000 machine with a $100 grinder. Espresso grind is ~0.5–0.75mm — similar to fine sand. Any particle-size variation causes channeling, under/over-extraction simultaneously.

**Target extraction:** 25–30 seconds from water contact. Balanced taste (not sour, not bitter).

---

## Stepped vs. Stepless

**Stepped**: Preset positions. Repeatable. Better for dialing in once and grinding the same beans for weeks.

**Stepless**: Adjust anywhere. Harder to repeat exact settings. Better for single-dosing different beans frequently.

For home use, stepped works fine. The Sette 270's stepped-within-stepless solves both.
