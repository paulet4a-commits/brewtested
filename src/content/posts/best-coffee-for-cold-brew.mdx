## Cold Brew Quick Guide

**Grind:** Coarse — sea salt sized. Fine grinds over-extract and turn bitter.

**Ratio:** 1 part coffee : 4 parts cold water

**Steep:** 12 hours at room temperature (8 minimum, 24 maximum)

**Strain:** Fine mesh or cheesecloth

**Dilute:** 1:1 with water or milk. Serve over ice.

**Storage:** Concentrate keeps 7–10 days refrigerated.
