The Breville Bambino Plus is a capable machine bottlenecked by one thing: the grinder you pair it with. A blade grinder or basic burr grinder will mask everything the Bambino can do. Match it with one of these five, and you'll be pulling shots that actually reflect the machine's quality.

## What the Bambino Plus Needs in a Grinder

The Bambino Plus uses a 54mm portafilter and a precision filter basket. It pulls at 9 bars through a ThermoJet heating system that's ready in 3 seconds. The machine is forgiving enough for beginners but precise enough that grind quality directly determines shot quality.

**What to look for:**
- Burr grinder only — blade grinders create particle inconsistency the Bambino cannot fix
- Stepless or fine-stepped adjustment — espresso requires micro-adjustments
- Consistent particle size — the Bambino's pressure profile exposes grind inconsistency

**Budget reality:** You don't need a $400+ grinder to make the Bambino sing. The sweet spot is $130–$200. The machine won't reveal the difference between a $200 and $600 grinder the way a Profitec or Rocket would.
