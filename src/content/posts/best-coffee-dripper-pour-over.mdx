## Pour-Over FAQ

**What grind size for pour-over?**
Medium-fine — think sea salt texture. Too coarse and water flows too fast (weak); too fine and it over-extracts (bitter).

**How long does brewing take?**
2.5–4 minutes from start to finish. The ritual is part of the appeal — more mindful than pushing a button.

**Are pour-overs better than automatic machines?**
More control, cleaner cup. Automatics are more convenient. Best setup: use both — automatic on busy mornings, pour-over when you want the full experience.
