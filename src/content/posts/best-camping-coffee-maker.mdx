## Camping Coffee FAQ

**What's the best method for backpacking?**
AeroPress Go for quality + versatility. GSI JavaDrip for ultralight. Both produce excellent coffee without electricity.

**How do I heat water without electricity?**
All five brewers need only hot water. Heat with a camp stove or Jetboil to 195–205°F (just off full boil), then brew normally.

**Can I make espresso camping?**
Yes — Wacaco Nanopresso generates 18-bar manually. Pack Nespresso capsules with the NS adapter for easiest setup.
