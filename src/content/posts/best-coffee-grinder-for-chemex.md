## Chemex Grinder FAQ

**What grind size should I use for Chemex?**

Chemex requires a medium-coarse grind, similar to sea salt. Most burr grinders hit this at settings 15-22 (for Baratza Encore) or 30-35 (for 50-setting grinders). Start there and adjust based on extraction time: 4-5 minutes is ideal.

**Can I use a blade grinder for Chemex?**

Blade grinders produce inconsistent particles that over-extract and muddy Chemex's clean profile. They work for immersion methods like French press but sacrifice Chemex's defining characteristic: clarity. Invest in an entry-level burr grinder (Encore) minimum.

**How often should I replace my grinder burrs?**

Quality burr grinders (Baratza, Fellow, Wilfa) last 500+ pounds of beans before meaningful wear. That's 5-10 years for home brewers. Conical burrs typically outlast the grinder's motor. You'll replace the entire grinder before needing new burrs.

**Should I grind right before brewing?**

Yes. Grind immediately before brewing to preserve volatile aromatics. Pre-ground coffee loses flavor within days. If you must grind ahead, store in an airtight container and use within 24 hours.
