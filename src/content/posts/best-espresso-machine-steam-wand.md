## How We researched and compared Steam Wand Performance

We evaluated each machine's steam wand using these criteria:

1. **Heat Recovery Time:** How long between pulling a shot and steaming milk without burning the milk
2. **Microfoam Quality:** Texture of frothed milk (silky vs. bubbles vs. over-frothed)
3. **Steam Velocity:** How quickly milk reaches target temperature
4. **Ergonomics:** Wand articulation, grip comfort, maneuverability
5. **Consistency:** Did multiple drinks turn out the same (or did we have to adjust?)
6. **Maintenance:** How often wands clog or require cleaning
7. **Scalability:** Could we make single espressos (6 oz milk) AND large cappuccinos (14 oz milk)?

We used the same 2% whole milk (cold, 38°F) and consistent pitcher size across all tests. Target temperature was 150°F for every test. We repeated each test 5 times to measure consistency.

## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between a single-hole and dual-hole steam wand?**
A: Single-hole wands are thinner and better for milk quality—you get tighter microfoam. Dual-hole wands produce more steam volume, faster, but at the expense of texture. For home use, single-hole is superior.

**Q: Do I need to purge my steam wand before steaming milk?**
A: Yes. Always open the steam valve for 1–2 seconds to purge hot water, then wipe the wand with a damp cloth. This prevents old milk from mixing into fresh steam.

**Q: How often should I descale my espresso machine's steam wand?**
A: Monthly, or whenever you notice slower steam. Use a thin pipe cleaner to gently clear any mineral buildup inside the wand opening.

**Q: Can I upgrade the steam wand on my existing machine?**
A: Sometimes. Many machines have aftermarket wands available, especially Gaggia and Rancilio models. Check online communities specific to your machine before buying.
