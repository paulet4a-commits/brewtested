## FAQ: Automatic Espresso Machines

**Q: How do super-automatic machines actually work?**
A: Super-automatic machines combine a burr grinder, water heating system, and pump into one unit. When you select a drink, the machine grinds beans to a preset grind size, tamps automatically, brews espresso through 15-bar pressure, and steams milk—all without manual intervention.

**Q: Can I use pre-ground espresso in a super-automatic machine?**
A: Some models allow it, but integrated grinders assume whole beans. Pre-ground coffee often leads to inconsistent tamping and channeling (water flowing through gaps). Stick with whole beans for best results.

**Q: How often do automatic espresso machines need descaling?**
A: Most require descaling every 200-500 shots, depending on water hardness. Jura and DeLonghi include descaling cycles that you trigger monthly or quarterly. Hard water accelerates mineral buildup, so consider a water filter or treatment.

**Q: What's the difference between semi-automatic and super-automatic?**
A: Semi-automatics require you to dose, tamp, and monitor extraction while automating just the pump. Super-automatics handle all steps automatically—one-touch brewing. Super-automatics are more convenient; semi-automatics offer more control.

**Q: Are built-in grinders as good as separate grinders?**
A: Integrated grinders are convenient but typically inferior to standalone burr grinders. For super-automatics, this is unavoidable—you're trading ultimate grinding quality for all-in-one convenience.

**Q: How much espresso can one automatic machine produce daily?**
A: Most handle 8-10 drinks per day comfortably. Heavy daily use (20+ drinks) may strain the heating system and require longer cooling periods between cycles. For large households, consider dual-boiler models like the Oracle Touch.
