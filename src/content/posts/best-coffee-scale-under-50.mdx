## Coffee Scale FAQ

**Do I really need a coffee scale?**
If you want consistent coffee, yes. Scoops are inaccurate — coffee density varies by roast and grind size. A scale eliminates this variable permanently.

**What's the ideal coffee-to-water ratio?**
The SCA recommends 1:15 to 1:17 (coffee:water by weight). Start at 1:16 and adjust to taste.

**What precision do I need?**
- Pour over / espresso: 0.1g ideal
- French press / drip: 1g sufficient
- Cold brew: 1g sufficient

**Do I need a timer on my scale?**
For pour over, yes — timing your pours helps with extraction consistency. For French press or cold brew, a phone timer works fine.
