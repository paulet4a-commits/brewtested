**Start with your milk routine.** The Bambino Plus offers automatic milk texturing as well as a manual mode. A Classic asks you to handle steaming yourself. That is a useful buying distinction; promised learning times, shot counts, and machine lifespans are not a sound basis for this decision.

## Identify the Classic before comparing

“Classic Pro” alone does not establish which revision a seller has. Gaggia North America's current product page describes the **Classic Pro E24**, with a brass boiler. Do not apply those specifications to an older Classic Pro or Evo listing without matching its model and revision. Confirm that a seller offering an E24 identifies that revision explicitly; the Classic Pro name alone is insufficient. [Gaggia North America model page](https://www.gaggia-na.com/products/gaggia-classic-pro).

## Choose for the routine you want

**Consider the Bambino Plus if** your priority is a milk drink with fewer manual steaming decisions. Its manual also explains how to steam manually, so the automatic setting does not prevent practice. You still prepare the coffee yourself; automatic milk does not mean automatic grinding and dosing. [BES500 manual](https://www.breville.com/content/dam/breville/us/en/assets/miscellaneous/instruction-manual/espresso/BES500-instruction-manual.pdf).

**Consider a Classic if** operating the steam wand yourself is part of what you want to learn. Read its brew-to-steam and steam-to-brew sequence before committing. Neither specification sheet proves which machine makes better-tasting espresso in your kitchen. We have no comparative taste test or documented lifespan study to rank them on those grounds.

## Budget for the complete setup

Before comparing totals, list the machine, any grinder you need, cleaning supplies, and delivery costs. Keep an optional accessory separate from a required purchase. Ask whether the supplied baskets suit the coffee you plan to use. Breville's manual distinguishes single-wall baskets for freshly ground beans from dual-wall baskets intended for pre-ground coffee.

Do not buy a grinder merely because another guide calls it an espresso grinder: confirm its intended brew methods and adjustment range. Our [beginner espresso guide](/posts/best-espresso-at-home-beginners-guide/) explains the workflow questions to ask.

## Before you order

1. Match model revision, voltage, and regional manual to the seller's listing.
2. Confirm the basket and milk-jug contents; measure space for filling and cleaning.
3. Check local warranty, returns, and access to service for that exact model.
4. Compare the complete checkout totals rather than an old machine-only price.
