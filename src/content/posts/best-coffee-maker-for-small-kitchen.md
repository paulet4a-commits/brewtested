## FAQ: Compact Coffee Makers for Small Kitchens

**Q: Can I fit a compact coffee maker in a dorm room?**
A: Absolutely. All five models tested fit comfortably on a dorm desk. The AeroPress Go and Nespresso Essenza Mini are especially dorm-friendly for their minimal footprint.

**Q: Is small coffee maker quality compromised vs. full-size models?**
A: Not necessarily. Brew quality depends more on heating elements and water distribution than size. The AeroPress Go and Nespresso Essenza Mini produce exceptional coffee despite compact dimensions.

**Q: What's the most affordable compact coffee maker?**
A: The Black+Decker CM0700B at around $25, followed by the AeroPress Go at $35. Both deliver solid performance for budget-conscious buyers.

**Q: Do compact coffee makers heat water as effectively as full-size machines?**
A: Most compact models heat just as effectively since they use similar thermal technology in smaller packages. The main difference is they hold less water, not that they heat water less efficiently.

**Q: Which compact maker is best for multiple people in one household?**
A: The Hamilton Beach 2-Way or Black+Decker 5-Cup can brew multiple cups. If you prefer [single-serve convenience](/posts/best-single-serve-coffee-maker/), the Keurig K-Mini allows quick individual cups back-to-back.
