## FAQ: Descaling Coffee Machines

### How Often Should I Descale My Coffee Maker?
Owner reviews and manufacturer specs suggest:
- **Hard water areas:** Every 4–6 weeks
- **Soft water areas:** Every 8–12 weeks
- **Visual cue:** If brew time slows by 10–15 seconds, it's time

Running filtered water through your machine daily reduces mineral buildup, extending the interval between descales.

### Will Descaler Damage My Machine?
Based on user reports, no—when used correctly. Follow the machine's manual timing (typically 15–30 minutes soak). Never leave descaler in the machine longer than recommended. Rinse thoroughly with fresh water (5+ brew cycles) to remove all chemical residue.

### Can I Use Vinegar Instead?
User feedback is mixed. Vinegar works for light deposits but leaves a persistent smell. Many owners report taste lingering for weeks after vinegar descaling. Dedicated descalers (like the 5 above) are formulated to rinse cleanly, making them worth the minimal cost. One reviewer tested both and switched to proper descaler for better results.

### Which Descaler Works in All Machines?
All five options work universally, but with nuance:
- **Impresa:** Reliable across brands, best value
- **Urnex Dezcal:** Eco-conscious, works everywhere
- **OXO:** Budget option, proven results
- **Durgol:** Premium, optimized for espresso (works in all, excels in espresso)
- **Keurig:** Official formula (works in Keurigs, unnecessary in others)
