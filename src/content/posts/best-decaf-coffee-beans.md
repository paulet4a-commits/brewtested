## FAQ

**Q: Why does decaf coffee taste flat?**
A: Most commercial decaf uses the indirect solvent method (ethyl acetate), which strips oils and flavor compounds. Swiss water process (Volcanica, some specialty brands) preserves more nuance by using water-based extraction.

**Q: How much caffeine is in decaf?**
A: Typically 2-15mg per 8oz cup. Regular coffee: 95mg. Decaf still provides a mild boost without the jitters.

**Q: What's the best grind for decaf?**
A: Buy whole bean, grind 30 minutes before brewing. Decaf oxidizes faster due to processing—fresh grind preserves remaining oils and aromatics.

**Q: Can I use decaf in espresso?**
A: Yes. Dark roasts (Kicking Horse, Death Wish) pull excellent shots. Lighter decafs may lack the body espresso demands.

**Q: How long does decaf last?**
A: Whole bean: 2-4 weeks in airtight container (room temp). Pre-ground: 1-2 weeks. Freeze for extended storage (up to 3 months in airtight bag).
