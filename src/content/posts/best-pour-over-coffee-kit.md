## FAQ

**Do I need a gooseneck kettle?**
Yes, for best results. A standard kettle makes pour over much harder—you can't control the water flow. The Chemex and V60 especially benefit from gooseneck kettles. See our [best pour over kettle guide](/posts/best-pour-over-kettle/) for affordable options.

**How many filters does each kit include?**
Chemex includes 100 filters. Hario V60 and Melitta each include 100 filters. Bodum includes 5 paper + 1 metal reusable. Fellow Stagg includes a standard amount. Check the product page for current counts.

**Can I use regular coffee filters?**
Only with the Melitta and Bodum kits. The Chemex requires specially sized filters, and the V60 works best with tabbed filters. Using the wrong filters wastes coffee and creates a mess.

**What grind size do I need?**
Pour over requires medium-fine grind—finer than French press, coarser than espresso. Ask your roaster or grind yourself if you have a burr grinder. Wrong grind size is the #1 reason pour over tastes bad.

**Which kit brews the fastest?**
The V60 and Melitta are fastest at 2.5-4 minutes. Chemex takes 4-5 minutes. Fellow Stagg is slower due to water heating time, but brewing is 3 minutes once water is ready. Bodum with metal filter is fast at 2-3 minutes.
