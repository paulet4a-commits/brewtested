## What Makes a Good Espresso Grinder?

**Consistency:** Uniform particle size = even extraction = balanced shots. Inconsistency means bitter over-extracted fines and sour under-extracted chunks simultaneously.

**Fineness range:** Espresso needs very fine grounds. Not all "burr grinders" reach true espresso fineness — blade grinders never do.

**Dial-in range:** More settings = finer adjustments = easier dialing. 60 settings (Breville) vs. 12 settings (Bodum) is a real difference for serious espresso.

**At $100, the Breville Smart Grinder Pro is the minimum grinder worth buying for dedicated espresso.** If $40 is the limit, the Mr. Coffee burr grinder beats any blade grinder but has a quality ceiling.
