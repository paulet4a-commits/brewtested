## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between French press sizes?**
A: Standard sizes are 3-cup (12oz), 8-cup (34oz), and 12-cup (51oz). Most home brewers use 8-cup presses because they brew enough for 2-4 cups of coffee while remaining easy to clean and store.

**Q: How do I prevent sediment in French press coffee?**
A: Use medium-coarse ground coffee (think sea salt texture). A finer grind produces more sediment. Allow settled grounds 30 seconds before pouring. Some presses have better filter systems than others—the 4-level filters in Secura and Frieling models minimize sediment significantly.

**Q: Can you use a French press with pre-ground coffee?**
A: Technically yes, but don't. Pre-ground coffee oxidizes quickly and loses flavor rapidly. Grinding immediately before brewing is the primary reason French press coffee tastes better than drip coffee.

**Q: Are glass or stainless steel French presses better?**
A: Both have trade-offs. Glass lets you watch brewing and is easier to clean, but breaks if dropped. Stainless steel is durable and retains heat longer, but you can't see progress. For durability: stainless. For observation: glass.

**Q: How often should I replace my French press?**
A: A quality press lasts 5-10+ years with normal use. You might replace the glass carafe or filter sooner. The Frieling model should last 15-20 years. Budget presses may need replacement after 3-5 years.
