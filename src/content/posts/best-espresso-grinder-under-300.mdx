## Buying Guide

### Burr Type: Conical vs. Flat

**Conical burrs** (Fellow Opus, Breville, Sette 270) run at lower RPM, generate less heat, and excel with light-medium roasts. The flavor profile tends toward sweetness and complexity. Good for filter + espresso dual use.

**Flat burrs** (Eureka Silenzio, Rancilio Rocky) produce more uniform particle size â€” more consistent extraction, brighter/cleaner flavor. Higher RPM means slightly more heat. Better for espresso-only use at this price tier.

### Adjustment: Stepless vs. Stepped

**Stepless** (Fellow Opus): infinite positions, maximum flexibility, rewards patience. Takes more shots to land. Best for bean rotators who need to re-dial weekly.

**Micro-stepped** (Sette 270 â€” 270 positions): combines stepless flexibility with physical click points for repeatability. Best of both approaches.

**Stepped** (Eureka, Breville, Rocky): fewer positions but easier to reproduce. Good for daily rituals where you pull the same bean repeatedly.
