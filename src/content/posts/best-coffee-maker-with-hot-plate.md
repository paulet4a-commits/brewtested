## FAQ: Common Questions About Coffee Makers with Hot Plates

**Q: How long can a hot plate keep coffee warm?**
A: Most hot plates safely keep coffee warm 1.5–2 hours. After 2 hours, flavors begin to degrade even with quality brewers. For longer periods, use a thermal carafe or pour into a separate insulated container.

**Q: Can a hot plate scorch coffee?**
A: Yes—prolonged heat causes coffee to break down chemically, creating bitter, burnt flavors. Ceramic carafes handle this better than glass. If you regularly keep coffee hot for extended periods, invest in a thermal carafe model.

**Q: Do I need a programmable coffee maker?**
A: Not necessarily. Programmable timers are convenient for morning routines but add to the cost. If you brew coffee at consistent times daily, a timer justifies the extra $20–30.

**Q: What's the difference between glass and thermal carafes?**
A: Glass carafes require a hot plate to maintain temperature but can scorch coffee. Thermal carafes use insulation instead—they keep coffee hot for hours without a hot plate, preventing scorching but making the carafe heavier and more expensive.

**Q: Can I use a coffee maker with the carafe removed?**
A: Never—most coffee makers require the carafe in place during brewing. Water will overflow and damage the brewer's internal components.
