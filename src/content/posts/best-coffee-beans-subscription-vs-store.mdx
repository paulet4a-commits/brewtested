## Freshness Reality Check

**Subscription roasted-to-order:** Peak freshness 7–14 days after roasting. Maintained for 21+ days via fast shipping.

**Local roaster:** Maximum freshness. Same-day roasting. Requires visiting a physical location.

**Specialty retail (Peet's, Stumptown):** 1–2 weeks old on arrival. Still quality — just not peak.

**Supermarket:** Often 4–8 weeks old before purchase. Acceptable for dark roasts, noticeable in lighter roasts.

---

## Which to Choose

**Choose subscription if:** You brew pour-over/AeroPress, want roasted-to-order freshness, or enjoy exploring new origins monthly.

**Choose store-bought if:** You want reliable quality without subscription commitment, have access to a local roaster, or prefer specific blends you can buy anytime.
