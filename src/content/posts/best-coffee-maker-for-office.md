## How We Tested

We placed each machine in a shared office environment and evaluated them over a full week, with multiple people using them daily. Here's what we measured:

**Volume & Capacity**: We tracked brew time and cup capacity to see how well each machine handled peak demand (morning arrival, post-lunch slump).

**Quiet Operation**: We used a decibel meter during brewing cycles to ensure machines didn't disrupt video calls or focus work. Office-appropriate machines stayed below 80 dB.

**Ease of Cleanup**: We timed how long cleanup took (no detailed disassembly—just what a typical user would do between brews) and checked how well water residue or grounds washed away.

**Brew Quality**: We brewed multiple pots and tested temperature consistency, flavor profile, and how well coffee stayed fresh when held in a carafe.

**Durability Under Use**: We ran aggressive daily cycles to identify machines that felt fragile or showed wear quickly. The winners survived a week of heavy office use without mechanical issues.

**User-Friendliness**: We had team members with zero prior experience operate each machine without instructions to gauge intuitiveness.

## FAQ

**Q: What's the ideal capacity for an office coffee maker?**
A: For 4-8 people, 10-12 cups is sufficient. For 8-15 people, 14 cups prevents bottlenecks. For 2-4 people, 8-10 cups is adequate. Larger teams should consider two machines or a machine with a fast brew cycle like the BUNN.

**Q: Should we get a machine with a thermal carafe or a heating plate?**
A: Thermal carafes keep coffee hotter longer without scorching it, making them better for offices where people grab coffee throughout the day. Heating plates are simpler but require you to use the coffee relatively quickly (within 1-2 hours).

**Q: How often should we clean the machine?**
A: Rinse the carafe and filter basket after each use (1-2 minutes). Deep clean the water reservoir monthly with a 1:1 vinegar-water solution to prevent mineral buildup. Machines without regular cleaning will develop bitter-tasting coffee within weeks.

**Q: Can a budget machine work for a large office?**
A: Yes, if you're willing to brew two consecutive pots during peak hours. The Hamilton Beach and BUNN are affordable and reliable enough to handle this workflow. For 12+ people, a larger-capacity machine like the Cuisinart prevents constant rebrewing.

**Q: What if someone on the team is a coffee snob?**
A: The Breville Precision Brewer is worth the investment if coffee quality matters to your team's morale. It's also a conversation starter and a sign that you value your team's comfort.
