## FAQ

**Q: Is instant coffee really made from real coffee?**
A: Yes. Instant coffee is brewed coffee with the water removed. The freeze-dry process (used by quality brands) preserves more flavor than the older spray-dry method.

**Q: Why does some instant coffee taste bad?**
A: Spray-dried instant (common in cheap brands) creates bitter, hollow flavors. Freeze-dried instant (Waka, Mount Hagen) tastes significantly better because it preserves more of the original coffee flavor.

**Q: Can instant coffee go stale?**
A: Yes. Once opened, instant coffee lasts about 3 months in an airtight container. Store away from light and heat to preserve flavor.

**Q: How much instant coffee per cup?**
A: Start with 1-2 teaspoons per 8 oz cup, depending on how strong you like it. Owner feedback suggests most people use about 1.5 teaspoons.

**Q: Is instant coffee worse than regular coffee?**
A: Quality instant coffee is comparable to drip coffee in caffeine content and flavor. The main trade-off is missing the ritual and complexity of freshly brewed coffee.

**Q: Can I use instant coffee in recipes?**
A: Absolutely. Instant coffee works great in desserts, sauces, and marinades. Use about 1 teaspoon of instant coffee powder per 1 tablespoon of water to create a "coffee extract."
