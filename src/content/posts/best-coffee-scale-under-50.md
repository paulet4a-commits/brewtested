## Coffee Scale FAQ

**Do I really need a coffee scale?**
If you want consistent coffee, yes. Scoops are notoriously inaccurate — coffee density varies by roast level and grind size. A scale eliminates this variable permanently.

**What's the ideal coffee-to-water ratio?**
The SCA (Specialty Coffee Association) recommends 1:15 to 1:17 (coffee:water by weight) for most brewing methods. Start at 1:16 and adjust to taste.

**Do I need a timer on my coffee scale?**
For pour over, yes — timing your pours helps with extraction consistency. For French press or cold brew, a phone timer works fine.

**What precision do I need?**
- Pour over / espresso: 0.1g precision ideal
- French press / drip coffee: 1g precision sufficient
- Cold brew: 1g precision sufficient

**Can I use a kitchen scale for coffee?**
Yes, especially for French press and drip coffee. For pour over and espresso, a dedicated coffee scale's faster response time makes a noticeable difference.
