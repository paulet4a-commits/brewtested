## Why Bottomless Portafilters Work

**Channeling detection** — water streams show immediately when pressure finds the path of least resistance. Fix tamping or distribution in real time.

**Crema evaluation** — even, thick brown ring = balanced extraction. Patchy = uneven packing or grind inconsistency.

**Grind quality check** — clumping and fines become visible in flow patterns.

**Learning acceleration** — visual feedback replaces guessing. Most users report dramatically faster skill development.
