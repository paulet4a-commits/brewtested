## FAQ

**How long does coffee stay hot in a thermal carafe?**
Most thermal carafe coffee makers keep coffee above 140°F (hot enough to drink) for 3-5 hours, depending on the model and room temperature. Premium models like the Moccamaster exceed 5 hours.

**Are thermal carafe coffee makers better than glass carafes?**
Yes—thermal carafes prevent burnt taste because there's no heating plate keeping coffee at high temperatures. Glass carafes rely on plates that can scorch flavor after 30-60 minutes.

**Can you use a thermal carafe from one coffee maker on another?**
Most carafes are proprietary to their machines, so mixing and matching typically doesn't work. Always verify compatibility before assuming interchangeability.

**Which thermal carafe coffee maker is easiest to clean?**
The OXO Brew and Ninja CE251 both have removable lids and are easier to clean than models where the carafe is permanently attached. Hand-washing is recommended for longevity.

**Do thermal carafe coffee makers brew faster?**
Not necessarily—brew time depends on the machine's heating and brewing system, not the carafe. Some thermal makers (like Moccamaster) brew slower for better extraction, while others (like Ninja) prioritize speed.
