## Beginner FAQ

**Do I need a separate grinder?**
Yes (for all non-Nespresso machines). Espresso requires fine, consistent grinding that pre-ground coffee can't achieve reliably. A $25–50 manual burr grinder is fine for learning. See our [best coffee grinder for beginners](/posts/best-coffee-grinder-for-beginners/) guide.

**What if my first shots are terrible?**
Expected. The first 5–10 shots are universally bad — you're learning grind size, tamp pressure, and timing simultaneously. This is normal.

**Should I buy a grinder before the machine?**
Recommended. A $80 grinder + $100 Stilosa beats a $15 blade grinder + $300 Bambino.
