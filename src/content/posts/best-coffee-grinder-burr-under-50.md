## FAQ

**Q: Is a burr grinder really necessary?**
A: If you care about coffee flavor, yes. Burr grinders produce consistent particle sizes, enabling uniform extraction. Blade grinders create inconsistent sizes that lead to uneven, unpredictable brewing results.

**Q: Can I use these for espresso?**
A: The Baratza Encore and Capresso reach espresso-fine settings, though they won't match dedicated espresso grinders' consistency. For true espresso machines, consider stepping up to a dedicated espresso burr grinder.

**Q: How often should I replace the burrs?**
A: Quality burrs in these models last 500-1000+ hours of grinding (roughly 3-5 years of daily home use). Baratza offers affordable burr replacements if you wear them out.

**Q: What's the difference between conical and flat burrs?**
A: Flat burrs produce slightly more uniform particle sizes and can grind finer, but conical burrs are typically quieter and easier to clean. For under-$50 grinders, the practical difference is minimal.

**Q: Should I grind all my beans at once?**
A: Never. Grind immediately before brewing. Pre-ground coffee oxidizes and loses aromatic compounds within 15-30 minutes of grinding, reducing flavor potential by 20-40%.
