## How to Brew Chemex

**You'll need:** Chemex, Chemex filter, gooseneck kettle, fresh-ground coffee, scale

**Recipe (6-cup / 30oz):**
- Coffee: 42g (medium-coarse grind — coarser than pour-over, finer than French press)
- Water: 700ml at 200°F
- Brew time: 4-5 minutes

**Steps:**
1. Fold filter into cone shape (one thick side facing the spout), rinse with hot water — pour out
2. Add ground coffee, make small indent in center
3. Start timer. Pour 80ml in a circular motion (bloom) — wait 45 seconds
4. Continue pouring in slow circles, keeping water level consistent
5. Aim for total brew time of 4-5 minutes
6. Remove filter. Pour immediately.

## Chemex FAQ

**What makes Chemex different from other pour-overs?**
The Chemex filter is 20-30% thicker than standard pour-over filters, removing more oils and fine particles than any other filter. The result is an extremely clean, bright, tea-like cup that's unique to the Chemex.

**What grind size for Chemex?**
Medium-coarse — between V60 (medium) and French press (coarse). If brewing too fast (under 3 minutes), go finer. If too slow or bitter (over 6 minutes), go coarser.

**Do I need a gooseneck kettle for Chemex?**
Recommended but not required. A gooseneck kettle gives you control over pour rate and saturation. See our [best pour over kettle](/posts/best-pour-over-kettle/) for recommendations.

**Chemex vs V60 — which is better?**
Chemex: cleaner cup, larger capacity, easier to share. V60: more control, faster, single-cup optimized. Both are excellent; choice depends on serving size and how much you value extraction control.

**Are Chemex filters compostable?**
Yes — Chemex natural unbleached filters are compostable. The white oxygen-bleached filters are also compostable and don't impart paper taste.
