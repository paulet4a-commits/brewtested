## FAQ

**Q: Can I get good coffee from a $20 maker?**
A: Yes. Owner reviews show these produce coffee as good as machines costing $50+ if you use quality beans and proper technique. The difference is usually convenience features, not brewing ability.

**Q: How long will a budget maker last?**
A: User feedback indicates 2-4 years of regular use is typical. Some last longer; some fail faster. But at $15-20, the cost-per-year is very reasonable.

**Q: Which should I pick if I drink alone?**
A: The **Black+Decker 5-cup** or **Primula Pour Over** both excel for solo drinkers. The 5-cup brews just enough without waste. The Primula gives you the best flavor if you have a kettle.

**Q: What about water quality?**
A: All these use basic reservoirs with no filtration. Water quality from your tap matters more at budget price points. If your water is hard, consider a cheap pitcher filter or using filtered water.

**Q: Are single-serve pod makers better at this price?**
A: Not really. Specs show pod brewers under $30 use lower-quality components. For the same $20 budget, a traditional drip maker gives better coffee per cup.
