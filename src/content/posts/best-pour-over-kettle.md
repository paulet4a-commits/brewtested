## Work Out What You Need

1. **Add all session water.** Include brewing, rinsing and warming, within the manual's fill limits.
2. **Choose presets or adjustment.** Write down your recipe temperatures before comparing buttons and dials.
3. **Check hold behavior.** Use the exact manual; a powered hold function is not passive insulation.
4. **Match the heat source.** Check stove compatibility or the electric base, regional voltage and plug.
5. **Compare delivered totals.** Include shipping and required accessories. This guide establishes no current cheapest model.

## What Brewing Temperature Should You Use?

Start with your coffee's or recipe's guidance rather than assuming every pour-over requires exactly 200 degrees F. For example, [OXO's manual, page 7](https://www.oxo.com/media/wysiwyg/BL_Brew_8717100_Adjustable_Temp_Pour_Over_Kettle_Booklet_M02.pdf) suggests 200 degrees F for coffee. That is a starting recommendation, not proof that another setting ruins a cup; adjust your process consistently as you learn your preference.
