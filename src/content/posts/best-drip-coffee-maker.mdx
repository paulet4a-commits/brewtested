## Drip Coffee FAQ

**What temperature should a drip coffee maker brew at?**
The SCA recommends 195–205°F (90–96°C). Machines that brew below 190°F under-extract — producing flat, weak coffee. Only the Breville and OXO on this list are certified to hit this range consistently.

**Glass vs thermal carafe — which is better?**
- **Glass + heating plate:** Keeps coffee warm indefinitely but continues cooking it — bitter after 30 minutes.
- **Thermal carafe:** Keeps coffee warm 2–4 hours without cooking it — better flavor preservation.

**How often should I clean my coffee maker?**
Descale every 1–3 months depending on water hardness. Hard water deposits reduce brew temperature accuracy over time.
