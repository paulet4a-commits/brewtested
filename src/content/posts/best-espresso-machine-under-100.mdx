## Check Legacy and Brand-Only Listings

Do not assume an ECMP50 listing includes a grinder. Match it to its manual and box contents using [Mr. Coffee's official manual finder](https://www.mrcoffee.com/instruction-manuals.html). For a listing labeled only “Sowtech Espresso,” request the exact model before comparing specifications or included parts.

## Before You Buy

1. **Decide how you will supply coffee.** Whole beans, pre-ground coffee, and capsules require different equipment and spending.
2. **Count the complete setup.** Confirm the filter holder, baskets, tamper, and any milk accessories included.
3. **Match the manual to the machine.** Follow its cleaning and descaling instructions; there is no universal monthly schedule here.
4. **Prefer a clear return route.** An unsupported used bargain can consume the remaining budget in parts.
5. **Skip an unsuitable offer.** More advertised bars or a stainless accent does not fix a missing accessory or an exceeded ceiling.
