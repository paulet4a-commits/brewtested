## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I really need all these accessories?**
A: The tamper is non-negotiable—it directly affects shot quality. The others enhance your workflow but aren't strictly necessary. Start with a tamper and shot glasses, then add others as you progress.

**Q: What size tamper do I need?**
A: Most home machines use 58mm baskets. Before buying, measure your portafilter basket diameter. Some machines use 54mm or 49mm, so verify first.

**Q: Can I use a regular glass for espresso shots?**
A: Technically yes, but it's not ideal. Regular glasses are thicker and don't show crema as well. Shot glasses are designed specifically for espresso's presentation and temperature profile.

**Q: How often should I clean my milk pitcher?**
A: After every use. Milk proteins dry quickly and are difficult to remove if left sitting. Rinse immediately and wipe dry. Weekly deep clean with espresso machine cleaner.

**Q: Will these accessories work with any espresso machine?**
A: Most of these are universal. The tamper size matters—check your basket diameter. Everything else (shot glasses, mat, pitcher) works with any machine.

**Q: What's the difference between steaming and frothing milk?**
A: Steaming heats the milk while creating microfoam (tiny bubbles). Frothing creates larger bubbles. A quality pitcher like the Rattleware lets you do both smoothly.
