## Coffee Storage FAQ

**How long do beans stay fresh in an airtight canister?**
3–4 weeks for whole beans. Ground coffee: about 2 weeks. After that, the beans won't go bad but flavor gradually fades.

**Should I refrigerate coffee beans?**
No. Temperature fluctuations cause condensation — moisture is the enemy of freshness. Cool, dark cabinet is ideal.

**Airtight vs. vacuum-sealed — what's the difference?**
Airtight = no air exchange. Vacuum-sealed = air actively removed, creating a more extreme oxygen-free environment. Vacuum (Fellow Atmos) is technically superior; a good airtight works well for most people.
