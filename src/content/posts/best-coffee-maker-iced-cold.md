## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does cold brew concentrate last?**
Most cold brew concentrate stays fresh 10-14 days in the refrigerator in an airtight container. After two weeks, oxidation begins degrading flavor. The Toddy system produces quantities that finish within this window.

**Can I use regular coffee maker grounds in a cold brew system?**
Yes. Cold brew is forgiving with grind size. Medium and medium-fine grinds work equally well. Avoid very fine espresso grinds (they over-extract) and very coarse grinds (under-extract). When in doubt, use your regular drip coffee grind.

**What's the coffee-to-water ratio for concentrate?**
The standard is 1:5 ratio (1 part coffee, 5 parts water). This produces concentrate you dilute with equal parts milk or water when serving. Adjust to 1:4 for stronger concentrate or 1:6 for lighter.

**Do iced coffee makers waste money compared to ice-in-regular-coffee?**
Iced coffee makers save money over time. Ice dilutes coffee as it melts, requiring more coffee per drink. Cold brew concentrate uses exactly what you need and stays fresh for days, reducing waste.
