## Noise Reference

- **Hand grinders**: 40–50 dB (whisper-level)
- **Premium electric burr** (Wilfa): ~60–65 dB
- **Standard electric burr** (Encore, OXO): ~70–75 dB
- **Blade grinders**: 90+ dB

For grinding without waking others: hand grinder or Wilfa. For "quiet enough": Encore or OXO.
