## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many cups does an office team actually need?**
A general rule: one cup per office worker at peak consumption. For an 8-person team, a 12-cup machine ensures everyone gets coffee, plus some carryover. If you have remote workers or staggered schedules, 10 cups may suffice. High-caffeine offices need 14+ cups.

**Should we choose a thermal or glass carafe?**
Thermal carafes keep coffee hotter for 4+ hours but can crack easily. Glass carafes break when dropped but stay cleaner longer. For offices with careful workers, glass is better. For fast-paced environments where carafes get knocked around, thermal wins. Ideally, choose a machine that includes both options.

**How often should office coffee makers be cleaned?**
Weekly descaling with a commercial descaling solution removes mineral buildup that ruins taste consistency. Office machines especially need regular cleaning because hard water mineral deposits accelerate with daily use. A neglected office brewer starts tasting noticeably worse after three weeks.

**Can we use pod-style coffee systems for offices?**
Pod systems like Keurig are convenient but expensive per cup ($0.50-1.00 per serving). Traditional drip brewers cost $0.05-0.15 per cup. For offices serving 20+ cups daily, traditional drip brewers save significant money. Use pods only for specialty drinks or offices with fewer than five daily users.

**What coffee should we stock for office brewing?**
Medium roasts work best in office settings because they're forgiving of varying water temperatures and brew times. Avoid dark roasts that taste bitter if over-brewed, and avoid light roasts that disappoint if under-extracted. Buy whole beans and grind fresh every few days for best results. Store beans in an airtight container away from light and heat.
