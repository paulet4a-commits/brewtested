## Buyer's Guide

**Carafe size:** 1–2 people → 4–5 cup. 2–4 people → 10–12 cup.

**Brew temperature:** Ideal is 195–205°F. Budget machines typically hit 185–195°F — acceptable but not optimal. Below 175°F and coffee will taste weak.

**Programmable timer:** The Hamilton Beach 49350 is the only sub-$50 machine on this list with a reliable 24-hour timer.

**Reusable vs. paper filter:** Reusable saves money; paper produces slightly cleaner coffee. Both are fine.
