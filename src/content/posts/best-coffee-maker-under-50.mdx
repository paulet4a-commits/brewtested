## A Practical $50 Buying Checklist

1. **Measure your usual drink in ounces.** A marketed cup count is not necessarily a count of your mugs. Compare the brewer's stated capacity with your own mug.
2. **Choose the workflow first.** A timer, a simple switch and a pod button solve different needs. Do not pay for connected features just to make a basic pot.
3. **Include the starter supplies.** Check whether the correct reusable filter is included or whether you need paper filters, pods or a separate basket.
4. **Use the delivered total.** Compare the exact model, item price, delivery, tax and required accessories against your $50 cap.
5. **Read the matching manual.** Check shutdown behavior, electrical requirements, cleaning instructions and whether the seller supplies the correct carafe.
