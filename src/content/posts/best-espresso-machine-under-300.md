## FAQ

**Q: Can I make good espresso with a machine under $300?**
A: Absolutely. The Breville Bambino Plus and De'Longhi Dedica both produce café-quality shots. You're trading some convenience features for better price, not quality.

**Q: What's the difference between semi-automatic and super-automatic?**
A: Semi-automatics require you to grind, tamp, and manually start/stop the extraction. Super-automatics handle grinding and extraction automatically. Semi-automatics offer better control; super-automatics save time.

**Q: Do I need a separate grinder?**
A: Yes, for all semi-automatics. These machines don't include grinders because integrated grinders (like the Mr. Coffee) can't achieve fine-enough grinds for espresso. Budget $60-150 for a quality burr grinder. See our [Best Burr Grinder Under $100](/posts/best-burr-grinder-under-100/) guide.

**Q: Which machine heats up fastest?**
A: The Breville Bambino Plus (3 seconds). The De'Longhi Dedica takes ~30 seconds. For most home use, 30 seconds is still convenient.

**Q: Will I need a water filter?**
A: Highly recommended. Hard water damages boilers and affects shot quality. A basic pitcher filter ($10-20) extends machine life and improves taste.
