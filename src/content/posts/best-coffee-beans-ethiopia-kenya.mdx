## Ethiopian vs. Kenyan: Key Differences

**Ethiopian:** Floral and fruity with lighter body — blueberry, jasmine, wine-like notes. Natural process varieties are most distinctive.

**Kenyan:** Brighter acidity with bold fruit — red currants, black currants, clean finish. AA grade is the quality benchmark.

Both are high-altitude origins. Ethiopian is more accessible for beginners; Kenyan rewards precision brewing.
