## Nespresso Pod FAQ

**What's the difference between Nespresso Original and Vertuo?**
Original uses 19-bar pressure like a traditional espresso machine — produces authentic espresso and ristretto. Vertuo uses centrifusion (spinning pod + water pressure) — produces a wider range of drink sizes but with a different crema profile. Pods are NOT interchangeable between systems.

**Can I use non-Nespresso pods in my Nespresso machine?**
For Original machines — yes, many third-party compatible pods exist (Lavazza, Starbucks, Peet's). For Vertuo machines — no, Nespresso uses proprietary barcode technology that only reads official Vertuo pods.

**How do I know if I have an Original or Vertuo machine?**
Check the capsule opening. Original capsules are small, round, and aluminum. Vertuo capsules are larger, dome-shaped, and have a barcode rim. The machine itself will say Original or Vertuo.

**Are Nespresso pods recyclable?**
Official Nespresso pods are aluminum and recyclable through Nespresso's free recycling program (available in most countries). Fill the provided bag and drop at a Nespresso boutique or UPS store. Third-party compatible pods vary by manufacturer.

**Which Nespresso pod has the most caffeine?**
Palermo Kazaar at intensity 12 has the highest caffeine among Original pods (~120mg per shot). For Vertuo, larger cup sizes naturally contain more caffeine due to volume.
