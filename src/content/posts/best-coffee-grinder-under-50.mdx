## Burr vs. Blade: The Real Difference

Burr grinders crush beans between two surfaces → uniform particle size → even extraction → better coffee.

Blade grinders chop randomly → powder AND chunks in the same batch → bitter fines + sour coarse particles together → muddy, inconsistent coffee.

**The OXO Brew at $50 is the minimum burr grinder worth buying.** If you can't stretch to $50, the Cuisinart DBM-8 at $35 is a genuine burr grinder that produces acceptable results.
