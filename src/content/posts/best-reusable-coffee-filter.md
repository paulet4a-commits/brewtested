## Reusable Filter FAQ

**Do reusable filters change coffee flavor?**
Yes — significantly. Metal filters allow coffee oils (cafestol, kahweol) to pass through, producing a fuller-bodied, richer cup. Paper filters trap these oils, producing a cleaner, brighter cup. Cotton filters behave more like paper.

**How do I clean a reusable coffee filter?**
Rinse immediately after use under running water to remove all grounds. Dishwasher weekly (for dishwasher-safe models). For cloth filters, boil every 2 weeks to remove trapped oils.

**How long do reusable coffee filters last?**
- Stainless steel: 5+ years with proper care
- Cloth: 6-12 months (then replace)
- Nylon mesh: 1-2 years

**Are reusable filters healthier?**
Research shows coffee oils (cafestol) raise LDL cholesterol. Paper-filtered coffee removes most of these oils. If you have cholesterol concerns, paper or cotton filters are preferable. Otherwise, the health difference is minimal.

**Which reusable filter fits my coffee maker?**
- **Basket-style drip machines (Cuisinart, Mr. Coffee, Hamilton Beach):** Goldtone basket
- **Flat-bottom drip machines (OXO, Bonavita):** RECAPS flat bottom
- **Chemex:** Able Kone
- **Hario V60:** Hario Stainless Dripper
- **Any pour-over cone:** CoffeeSock (sized to your cone)
