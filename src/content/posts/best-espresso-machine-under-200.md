## Espresso Machine FAQ

**What pressure does espresso need?**
9 bars is the standard for espresso extraction — this is what professional machines use. Higher pump ratings (15-bar, 20-bar) are marketing numbers for maximum capability; what matters is that the machine maintains consistent 9-bar pressure during extraction.

**Do I need a grinder for an espresso machine?**
Yes — if using ground coffee (not pods). Espresso requires a very fine, consistent grind that pre-ground coffee can't achieve reliably. Pre-ground espresso goes stale within 1-2 weeks. See our [best burr grinder under $100](/posts/best-burr-grinder-under-100/) for grinder recommendations.

**What's the difference between semi-automatic and super-automatic espresso machines?**
Semi-automatic: you grind, tamp, and control extraction time. Full control, requires learning. Super-automatic: machine does everything — grind, tamp, brew, clean. Less control, more convenience, much higher cost ($500+).

**Can I make lattes with a home espresso machine?**
Yes — if the machine has a steam wand. You need to froth milk separately and combine with your espresso shot. The Mr. Coffee Café Barista automates this. For manual machines, see our [best milk frother](/posts/best-milk-frother/) guide.

**How long do home espresso machines last?**
Budget machines ($100-200): 2-5 years with regular cleaning. Mid-range ($300-600): 5-10 years. Premium ($1000+): 10-20 years. Descaling every 2-3 months significantly extends lifespan.
