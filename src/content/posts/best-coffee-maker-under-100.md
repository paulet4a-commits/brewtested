## Coffee Maker FAQ

**Is an SCA-certified coffee maker worth it?**
Yes — SCA certification means the machine reliably brews between 195-205°F, which is the optimal range for extraction. Non-certified machines often brew at 185-190°F, producing flat, weak coffee. The Bonavita and OXO both achieve this at under $100.

**What's the difference between a glass and thermal carafe?**
Glass + heating plate: keeps coffee warm but continues "cooking" it — bitter after 20-30 minutes. Thermal carafe: keeps coffee hot 2+ hours without applying heat, preserving flavor. For best coffee, choose thermal.

**How many cups does a coffee maker need to make for me?**
- Solo drinker: 8-cup is sufficient
- Two coffee drinkers: 10-12 cup
- Family of 4+: 12-14 cup

**Do I need a programmable coffee maker?**
Only if you want coffee ready when you wake up. If you brew on demand, a simple one-button machine (like the Bonavita) actually produces better coffee since you can use freshly boiled water temperature.
