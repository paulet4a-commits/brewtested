## FAQ

**Can handheld frothers create latte art?**
Zulay and Bonsenkitchen produce fine enough microfoam for basic latte art. Coarser frothers (PowerLix) won't work for detailed designs.

**Which milk froths best?**
Whole cow's milk is easiest. Oat milk works well with stronger motors. Almond and soy are trickier — lower protein resists frothing.

**Can you froth cold milk?**
Yes, all battery-powered models work with cold milk. Aeroccino3 has a dedicated cold foam mode.
