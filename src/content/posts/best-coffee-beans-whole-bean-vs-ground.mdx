## The Grinder: Essential for Whole Bean

If you choose whole bean, a burr grinder is non-negotiable. Blade grinders create inconsistent particle sizes — some dust, some chunks — causing uneven extraction.

## The Verdict

**Choose whole bean if:** You drink coffee daily, freshness matters, you don't mind 30 seconds of grinding, and you have counter space.

**Choose ground if:** You drink occasionally, convenience is the priority, or you're new to specialty coffee and not ready to invest in a grinder.

**The math:** At 1–2 weeks post-roasting, whole bean tastes 15–25% better than ground in blind tasting. The difference is most obvious in lighter roasts and single-origin coffees.
