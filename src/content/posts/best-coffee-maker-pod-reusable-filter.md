## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Are reusable K-Cup filters safe to use?**
A: Yes, absolutely. All recommended filters use food-grade materials certified BPA-free. The Solofill V4 is particularly well-tested with millions of units sold. Always verify your specific Keurig model compatibility before purchasing.

**Q: How long does a reusable K-Cup filter last?**
A: Quality filters like Solofill last 2-3 years with proper care. Budget options like SCISHARE typically last 1-2 years. Lifespan depends on material (stainless steel > silicone) and cleaning practices. Hand washing extends life significantly.

**Q: Can I use any ground coffee in my Keurig with a reusable filter?**
A: You can use medium to coarse ground coffee. Very fine espresso grinds may escape the filter. Use a standard drip grind or buy pre-ground medium roasts for best results. Avoid dark French roasts which can make the cup bitter in K-Cup systems.

**Q: Is it really cheaper to use reusable filters instead of K-Cups?**
A: Yes, dramatically. A quality reusable filter costs $20-40 and lasts 2+ years. K-Cups cost $0.50-1.00 each. One filter breaks even after 30-50 cups. A daily coffee drinker saves $200-300 per year using reusable filters and bulk ground coffee.

**Q: What's the environmental impact of switching to reusable filters?**
A: Significant. The average K-Cup user generates 150-200 plastic cups per year. Switching to reusable filters eliminates that waste. Stainless steel filters are fully recyclable at end of life. One filter prevents hundreds of pods from reaching landfills.
