## How We researched and compared These Kettles

Our research focused on five key metrics for pour over brewing:

1. **Temperature Precision** – Measured actual water temperature vs. displayed/intended temperature using calibrated thermometers
2. **Boil Time** – Tracked time from cold water to 212°F using consistent amounts (1L)
3. **Pour Control** – Evaluated gooseneck spout design, balance, and ability to maintain consistent flow rates
4. **Durability** – Assessed build quality, seam construction, and handle integrity after extended use
5. **User Experience** – Tested ease of refilling, weight, and comfort during actual pour over brewing sessions

All kettles were tested with standard medium-roast filter coffee and water from the same source to ensure consistency.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Do I really need an electric gooseneck kettle, or will stovetop work fine?
**A:** Both work great for pour over. Electric kettles offer convenience and precise temperature control, while stovetop kettles are simpler and more affordable. Choose based on your brewing ritual preference and budget.

### Q: What's the ideal temperature for pour over coffee?
**A:** Most specialty coffees brew best at 195°F-205°F. Boiling water (212°F) works fine for darker roasts. The Bonavita and Fellow's temperature control help you dial in the sweet spot for your specific beans.

### Q: Can I use these kettles for tea or other hot beverages?
**A:** Absolutely. All kettles listed work equally well for tea, hot chocolate, and other beverages. The gooseneck design benefits any hot water pouring task.

### Q: Which kettle has the best warranty?
**A:** Bonavita and Fellow both offer strong warranties (typically 1-2 years). Check the current Amazon listing for warranty details on each product, as these can change.

### Q: How long does water stay hot in these kettles?
**A:** Electric kettles with keep-warm functions (Cosori, Bonavita, Fellow) maintain temperature for 30 minutes. Stovetop kettles cool over time—you may need to reheat between pours on larger brewing sessions.
