## Gifting Tips

**Pair complementary items:** A Hario V60 ($25) + Baratza Encore ($50) together ($75 total) beats either alone. You're giving a complete upgrade path.

**Include a gift message about use:** "This makes coffee you can truly taste" resonates differently than just handing over equipment.

**Know their setup:** If they already have:
- A good grinder → give beans or brewing equipment
- A brewing method → give a grinder or kettle
- Basic equipment → give premium beans or a new brewing style

**Avoid duplicates:** Gently find out what they already own. Many coffee lovers accumulate duplicate brewers. A grinder or kettle solves this problem.
