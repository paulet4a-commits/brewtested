## What Matters More Than a “Best” Badge

1. **Your normal batch:** Choose capacity for the coffee you actually drink, using the maker's serving convention.
2. **The controls:** Decide whether a switch is enough or you need documented programming and timed shutoff.
3. **The carafe:** Confirm glass versus thermal and whether a replacement is available. Do not infer carafe type from trim color.
4. **Recurring supplies:** Include filters and the cleaning products specified by the manual in your own budget.
5. **The full offer:** Check shipping, taxes, included parts, seller condition, and return terms.
