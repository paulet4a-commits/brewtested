## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need to spend more than $30 to get good coffee?**
A: Not at all. These budget coffee makers produce excellent coffee comparable to machines costing 2-3x more. The difference at this price point comes down to capacity and features, not quality.

**Q: Which 5-cup model should I choose?**
A: The BLACK+DECKER CM0915 edges out Mr. Coffee due to faster brew time and the reliable warming plate. If budget is your absolute priority, Mr. Coffee is still excellent at $21.99.

**Q: Should I get a 5-cup or 12-cup model?**
A: Get a 5-cup if you live alone or with one other person and drink coffee within an hour of brewing. Choose 12-cup if you have a household of multiple coffee drinkers or enjoy coffee throughout the morning.

**Q: Can budget coffee makers make espresso or specialty drinks?**
A: No. These are traditional drip machines. They make regular black coffee and are not suitable for espresso, lattes, or cappuccinos. Check out our [best automatic drip coffee maker](/posts/best-automatic-drip-coffee-maker/) guide for programmable options.

**Q: How long do these coffee makers last?**
A: With regular cleaning and descaling every 3-4 months, most budget machines last 3-5 years. They're built simpler than expensive models, so they're actually very reliable.
