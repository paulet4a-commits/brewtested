## Why Pour Over Needs Different Beans Than French Press

French press steeps — darker roasts and oily beans perform better with full immersion body. Pour over percolates — lighter and medium roasts reveal origin character, brightness, and complexity that immersion extracts muddy. Single-origin beans shine here because blend recipes are built for espresso and milk drinks, not filter clarity.
