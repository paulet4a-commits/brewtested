## FAQ

**Q: Is a glass carafe better than a thermal carafe?**

Glass lets you see your coffee level and looks elegant, but it requires a warming plate (which can burn coffee over time). Thermal carafes keep coffee hot longer without heat, making them ideal if you sip slowly. For most people, glass + decent brewing temperature wins on taste. Check out [our guide to finding the best drip coffee maker](/posts/best-drip-coffee-maker/) for more details.

**Q: What size should I buy?**

If you live alone or drink one cup, 4-5 cups suffices. For couples, 8-10 cups is ideal. For families or daily entertaining, 12+ cups makes sense. Remember: larger machines take longer to heat water.

**Q: How often should I replace the carafe?**

A quality glass carafe lasts 3-5 years with normal use. If you see visible cracks, clouding, or thermal stress marks, replacement time has come. Most manufacturers sell replacement carafes for $15-30.

**Q: Do I need a water filter built into the coffee maker?**

If your tap water is very hard or chlorinated, yes. Built-in filters (like Bonavita) remove sediment and odors. If you already use a filtered pitcher, you can skip it. For soft water areas, it's less critical.

**Q: Should I buy a programmable model?**

If you want fresh coffee waiting when you wake up, absolutely. If you're home and can wait 6-10 minutes, a non-programmable model saves money and often brews better (simpler design, fewer failure points).
