## Single Serve Coffee Maker FAQ

**Are single serve coffee makers worth it?**
For solo drinkers or mixed-preference households, yes — you eliminate wasted coffee and get exactly what you want, when you want it. The convenience premium is real.

**K-Cup vs. ground coffee — which is better?**
Ground coffee always wins on quality and cost per cup. But K-Cups win on convenience and variety. The Hamilton Beach FlexBrew lets you have both.

**How much do K-Cups cost per cup?**
K-Cups average $0.40-$0.80 per pod. Ground coffee averages $0.10-$0.25 per cup. Over 500 cups/year, K-Cups cost $100-200 more.

**Do single serve machines make espresso?**
No. Single serve pod machines brew standard coffee at low pressure. For espresso you need a dedicated espresso machine — see our [best espresso machine under $100](/posts/best-espresso-machine-under-100/) guide.

**What K-Cup pods taste best?**
Taste is personal, but highly rated picks include: Death Wish Coffee, Starbucks Pike Place, Green Mountain Breakfast Blend, and Dunkin' Original Blend.

**Can I use reusable K-Cup pods?**
Yes — the Keurig My K-Cup Universal Reusable Filter works in all Keurig machines including the K-Mini. Fill with any ground coffee for significant cost savings.
