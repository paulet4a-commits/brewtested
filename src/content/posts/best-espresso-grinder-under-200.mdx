## Key Buying Factors

**Stepless vs. stepped adjustment:** Stepless lets you dial to any position. Stepped grinders click between fixed positions. For espresso, stepless (or micro-stepped like the Sette) is strongly preferred.

**Flat vs. conical burrs:** Flat burrs produce more uniform particle sizes → brighter, more consistent shots. Conical burrs are quieter and easier to clean. Both work well for home espresso.

**Single dose vs. hopper:** Single dose (DF64) means you weigh beans before each grind — more precision, zero stale beans. Hopper grinders (Baratza, Eureka) are faster for daily use.
