## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between insulated and regular mugs?**  
A: Double-wall vacuum insulation creates an air gap that thermally isolates your beverage from external temperatures. Regular mugs conduct heat directly; insulated mugs retain temperature 6-12x longer.

**Q: How long should coffee stay hot in a quality mug?**  
A: Premium mugs (Yeti, Hydro Flask) keep coffee drinkable (140°F+) for 6+ hours. Budget options (Contigo) maintain heat for 3-4 hours. For all-day retention, Stanley's 12+ hours is exceptional.

**Q: Why do lids matter so much?**  
A: Lids prevent evaporative cooling and reduce air circulation. A poor seal loses 30% more heat than a quality vacuum mug without a lid. Contigo's seal is superior for spill prevention; Yeti/Hydro Flask prioritize ease of use.

**Q: Can I use these mugs for cold beverages?**  
A: Absolutely. The same vacuum technology works backwards—ice water stays cold for 12+ hours in Yeti or Hydro Flask.

**Q: Are premium mugs worth the price?**  
A: Yes, if you value convenience. A $35 Yeti Rambler costs $0.10 per use over three years—cheaper than replacing lukewarm coffee by 10 AM. For casual users, Stanley's $25 option is economical.
