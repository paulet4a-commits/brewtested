## Moka Pot FAQ

**How much coffee does a moka pot make?**
A "3-cup" moka pot makes about 6oz of concentrated coffee — enough for 1–2 demitasse cups or one large Americano. A "6-cup" makes about 12oz.

**Is moka pot coffee the same as espresso?**
Not exactly. Moka pots brew at 1–2 bars of pressure vs. 9 bars for espresso machines. The result is strong and concentrated — similar flavor profile but without true espresso crema.

**What grind size for moka pot?**
Medium-fine — coarser than espresso but finer than drip. Pre-ground espresso blends work well.

**Can I use a moka pot on an induction cooktop?**
Only stainless steel moka pots work on induction. Aluminum moka pots (including Bialetti) require an induction adapter disk.
