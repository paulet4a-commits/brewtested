## Frequently Asked Questions

**What temperature should coffee brewing water reach?**

Optimal brewing temperature is 195-205°F (90-96°C). Water below 195°F under-extracts and produces sour, thin coffee. Water above 205°F over-extracts and creates bitter flavors. The best automatic machines maintain this temperature throughout the entire brew cycle, not just at the start.

**Is a thermal carafe better than a heat plate?**

Yes, thermal carafes preserve coffee quality much longer. Heat plates continue heating after brewing stops, which scorches coffee and develops unpleasant burnt flavors after 30-45 minutes. Thermal carafes insulate the coffee, keeping it hot without additional heat. Your coffee tastes better at hour three with a thermal carafe than it does at hour one with a heat plate.

**How do I choose between programmable and non-programmable?**

Choose programmable if you want fresh coffee ready when you wake up or return home. Non-programmable machines are simpler and more reliable (fewer electronic components to fail). Most serious coffee enthusiasts prefer non-programmable machines because they allow precise control over brewing parameters.

**What's the difference between drip and pour-over brewing?**

Drip machines automate the pouring process—you load grounds and water, and heating elements control temperature and timing. Pour-over requires manual water pouring with a kettle, giving you complete extraction control but demanding more attention. Pour-over typically produces slightly cleaner, brighter cup quality if you nail the technique.

**Can I use a coffee maker for tea brewing?**

Technically yes, but it's not recommended. Coffee makers are designed for oil-based (coffee) extraction, not leaf-based (tea) infusion. The temperature cycles optimized for coffee aren't ideal for different tea types. Use a dedicated electric kettle for tea.

**How often should I clean my coffee maker?**

Daily: rinse the filter basket and carafe after each use. Weekly: run a cycle with white vinegar to remove mineral buildup. Monthly: deep clean the shower head and internal components if you have hard water. Hard water areas should descale more frequently (every 2-3 weeks).
