## The Integrated Grinder: Honest Assessment

**What works:** 16 grind settings cover the full range. Low retention (~0.5g). Robust — users report 3+ years of reliable operation. You can adjust mid-grind.

**What doesn't:** Inconsistency increases at finest settings — some larger particles slip through. Grinding is slow (30–40 seconds). Noticeably below a dedicated $250+ grinder in shot-to-shot consistency.

**Reality:** Most users dial in acceptable espresso shots. You'll get decent crema, reasonable body, enjoyable flavor. Not laser-precise, but consistently good enough that you'll make espresso regularly.

## Real Owner Complaints

1. **Loud grinder** — 30–40 seconds, wakes light sleepers in apartments
2. **Dialing-in patience required** — first 10–15 shots usually aren't perfect
3. **Proprietary portafilter basket** — limited third-party basket compatibility
4. **Thermal cycling** — 20–30 second wait between espresso and steam
5. **Durability after 3+ years** — some machines show wear; repair costs $150–300 post-warranty
