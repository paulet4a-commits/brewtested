## Grind Guide for Pour Over

**Target:** Medium — sea salt texture. Water flows in 3–4 minutes total.

- **Too fine**: water flows slowly, bitter/over-extracted
- **Too coarse**: water flows fast, weak/sour
- **Adjust**: one click at a time, taste each brew
