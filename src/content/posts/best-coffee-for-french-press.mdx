French press needs coffee that can handle 4 minutes of full immersion. No paper filter means oils and fines pass through — light roasts taste hollow and sour. Medium to dark roasts shine here. Here are 5 tested picks.

## The Rule: Coarse Grind Only

**Ratio:** 1g coffee : 15g water. For a 34oz (1000ml) French press: 65g coffee, 1000g water.

Fine grounds cloud the cup and over-extract. Sea salt-sized coarse grind is non-negotiable. Always whole bean — grind right before brewing.

## Brewing Checklist

1. **Coarse grind** — sea salt sized
2. **Water 195–205°F** — not boiling
3. **Bloom:** Pour 1cm of water, wait 30 seconds, then fill
4. **Steep 4 minutes** — adjust ±30 seconds to taste
5. **Press slowly** — 30 seconds of steady pressure
6. **Decant immediately** — leaving coffee on grounds over-extracts
