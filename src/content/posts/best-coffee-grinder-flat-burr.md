## Introduction

## Flat Burr Grinder FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between flat and conical burrs?**

A: Flat burrs produce more uniform particle sizes, which translates to more consistent extraction in espresso. Conical burrs are more forgiving and work well for filter coffee. Flat burrs are the choice for espresso precision.

**Q: Are flat burr grinders worth the extra cost?**

A: Yes, especially for espresso. The consistency improvement directly impacts shot quality and reduces the frustration of dialing in. For filter coffee, a good conical burr grinder is sufficient.

**Q: How often should I replace flat burr grinder burrs?**

A: Expect 500–1000 kg of grinding before noticeable wear. Most quality flat burr grinders have easily replaceable burrs available for $20–60, making long-term ownership economical.

**Q: Can I use a flat burr grinder for all brewing methods?**

A: Absolutely. Flat burrs excel at espresso but also produce excellent results for pour-over, french press, and Turkish coffee. You get precision across the board.
