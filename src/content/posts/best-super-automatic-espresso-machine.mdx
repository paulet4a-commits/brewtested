## Is a Super-Automatic Worth It?

Super-automatics shine for households where multiple people drink different coffee styles, or where convenience is valued over craft. They're not ideal for espresso purists — the best manual machines produce better extraction ceiling.

**Buy super-auto if:** Convenience, low skill requirement, and freshly ground coffee daily matter most.

**Buy semi-auto if:** You want to develop technique and reach the highest possible shot quality.
