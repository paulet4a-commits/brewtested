## Hand vs. Electric: Which to Choose

**Choose hand grinding if:** you brew 1–2 cups daily, value the ritual, travel frequently, have limited counter space, or are on a tight budget.

**Choose electric if:** you brew 3+ cups daily, want timer convenience, need to grind for multiple people, or prefer speed over ritual.
