French press is the most forgiving immersion brewing method — no paper filters, no precise pour rates, just coarsely ground coffee steeped in hot water. Done right, it produces the richest, most full-bodied cup of any manual brewer.

## The Perfect French Press Recipe

| Variable | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Coffee | 30g (~4 tablespoons) |
| Water | 500ml at 200°F |
| Grind | Coarse (sea salt texture) |
| Steep time | 4 minutes |
| Yield | 2 cups |

**Method:** Preheat the press → add coffee → pour all water → stir gently → place lid plunger-up → press at 4 minutes → pour immediately.

## French Press FAQ

**Why is my French press bitter?**
Over-extraction — too-fine a grind or too-long a steep. Use coarse grind, keep to 4 minutes, pour immediately.

**Why does it taste weak?**
Under-extraction — too-coarse grind, too-cool water, or too little coffee. Use 60g per liter, 200°F water.

**Why so much sediment?**
Standard mesh filters allow fine particles through. Fix: coarser grind, slower press, let cup sit 30 seconds before drinking, or upgrade to Espro P6.
