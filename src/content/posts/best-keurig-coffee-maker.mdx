## FAQ

**K-Cup vs. reusable filter — which is better?**
K-Cups win on convenience and variety (~500+ options). Reusable filters win on cost (~$0.20/cup vs. $0.40–0.80) and let you use any ground coffee. The My K-Cup reusable filter works with all models.

**Does Keurig coffee taste worse than drip?**
Yes, generally — K-Cups use less coffee per oz and brew at lower pressure. The K-Elite's strong mode closes the gap significantly. For serious coffee quality, see our [best drip coffee maker](/posts/best-drip-coffee-maker/) guide.
