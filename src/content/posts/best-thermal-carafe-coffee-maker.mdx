## FAQ

**How long does a thermal carafe keep coffee hot?**
Premium vacuum carafes (Moccamaster, OXO, Cuisinart DCC-3200): 3–4 hours at full temperature. Budget thermal (Hamilton Beach, Ninja): 1.5–2 hours. All significantly better than a heating plate, which starts degrading coffee flavor in 20–30 minutes.

**Glass carafe vs. thermal — which is better?**
Thermal wins if you drink coffee slowly or in batches. Glass wins if you drink the whole pot within 30 minutes and prefer lighter weight.
