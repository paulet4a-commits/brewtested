## Nespresso Vertuo Pods FAQ

**What's the difference between Vertuo and original line pods?**

Vertuo uses centrifusion technology (spins capsules at 7,000 RPM) for larger cups (3.7-5 oz) and different extraction than the original line (which uses 19-bar pressure for smaller, traditional espresso). Vertuo pods physically don't fit original machines and vice versa.

**Can I recycle Nespresso Vertuo pods?**

Yes. Nespresso operates a free recycling program. Mail back used pods in provided bags, or drop at participating retailers. Aluminum is infinitely recyclable, and grounds become compost. This matters if sustainability drives your purchase.

**Which pods work best with milk?**

Melozio and Espresso blend beautifully with milk because their balanced profiles don't overpower dairy or alternatives. Ristretto works if you want a strong flat white. Voltesso excels with oat milk (the acidity complements grain notes).

**How long do Nespresso Vertuo pods stay fresh?**

Unopened boxes maintain peak freshness for 2 years from manufacture date. Once opened, use within 2-3 months for best flavor. Store in cool, dry places—heat and humidity degrade aromatics quickly. Vacuum-sealed bags stay fresh longer than open boxes.

**Are there subscription discounts?**

Nespresso offers 10-20% discounts for auto-replenishment subscriptions. The trade-off: less flexibility to experiment. We recommend one-time purchases while exploring, then subscribing to your favorites once preferences crystallize.
