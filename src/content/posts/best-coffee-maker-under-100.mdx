## Is SCA Certification Worth It?

Yes. SCA certification means the machine reliably brews between 195–205°F — the optimal extraction range. Non-certified machines often brew at 185–190°F, producing flat, weak coffee. The Bonavita and OXO both achieve professional brew standards for under $100. For most people, this is the most meaningful spec to check.

**Glass vs thermal carafe:** Thermal wins on flavor every time. Glass + heating plate keeps coffee warm but continues cooking it — bitter after 20–30 minutes. Thermal keeps coffee hot 2+ hours without heat.
