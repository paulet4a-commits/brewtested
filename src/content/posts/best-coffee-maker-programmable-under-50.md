## FAQ

**What does "programmable" mean on a coffee maker?**
A programmable coffee maker includes a timer that lets you set when brewing should start. You load it the night before with water and grounds, set the timer for your wake-up time, and fresh coffee brews automatically—no manual intervention required.

**How long should a programmable coffee maker last?**
Most quality coffee makers last 5-7 years with proper care. The warmplate is often the first component to degrade. Models in this price range typically match that lifespan, with some lasting longer if used gently.

**Can I use a programmable coffee maker with my preferred coffee beans?**
Absolutely. Programmable coffee makers work with any ground coffee that fits standard basket filters. The timer function doesn't affect your choice of beans at all—it only controls when brewing starts.

**Are programmable coffee makers worth the extra cost?**
If you have a consistent daily schedule, a programmable coffee maker justifies its cost through convenience. Morning preparation time shrinks to zero, and you gain the luxury of waking to fresh coffee. For irregular schedules, the extra cost may not be worth it.

**What's the difference between a warmplate and thermal carafe?**
A warmplate heats from below and can scorch coffee if left too long. A thermal carafe is insulated and keeps coffee hot for 2-3 hours without heat, preventing flavor degradation. Warmplate models are cheaper but require more attention.
