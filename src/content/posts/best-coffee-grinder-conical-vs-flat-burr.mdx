## Conical vs. Flat: The Science

**Conical advantages:** Lower heat, fewer ultra-fine particles (better for French press), more forgiving with varying bean densities.

**Flat advantages:** Extremely uniform particle size, minimal fines for cleaner extractions, superior filter coffee clarity.

**The rule:** Pour-over → flat burrs. French press/espresso → conical burrs are fine. If you only brew one method, match the burr type. If you brew multiple, conical is more versatile.
