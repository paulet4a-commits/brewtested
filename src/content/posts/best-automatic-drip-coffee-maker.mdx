## FAQ

**What brew temperature should a drip maker reach?**
195–205°F — the SCA standard. Budget machines often fall short; the Moccamaster and Breville both hit this range consistently.

**Do I need a burr grinder for drip coffee?**
Yes. Blade grinders produce uneven particles — some over-extract (bitter), some under-extract (weak). A burr grinder is the best upgrade after the machine.
