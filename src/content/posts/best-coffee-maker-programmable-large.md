## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What's the difference between a thermal carafe and a warming plate?**
A: A thermal carafe is a double-walled insulated container that keeps coffee hot without electricity. A warming plate sits under the pot and generates heat. Thermal carafes are better long-term because warming plates can eventually scorch coffee if it sits too long. If you need coffee to stay hot for 2+ hours, choose thermal.

**Q: How often should I clean my programmable coffee maker?**
A: Weekly rinsing of the carafe and filter basket is essential. Monthly descaling with white vinegar keeps the internal heating elements clean. The Cuisinart has an automatic self-cleaning cycle. Most models need manual descaling every 3-6 months depending on water hardness.

**Q: Can I use these for cold brew or espresso?**
A: No, these are designed for hot drip coffee only. They heat water to 195-205°F, which is wrong for espresso. For cold brew, you need a different system. These also won't work for pour-over setups.

**Q: What grind size should I use?**
A: Use medium grind for all these machines. Too coarse and you'll get weak coffee; too fine and it'll be over-extracted and bitter. Pre-ground coffee labeled "drip coffee" is your safest bet.

**Q: How long do these machines typically last?**
A: Budget models ($45-70) usually last 2-3 years. Mid-range machines ($89-150) often reach 4-5 years. Premium models like the Moccamaster can last 7-10 years with proper maintenance.
