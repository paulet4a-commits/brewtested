Coffee gifts are either genuinely useful or completely ignored. The difference is knowing what level of coffee drinker you're buying for. A basic drip machine owner doesn't need a precision pour-over kettle. A specialty coffee enthusiast will be unimpressed by flavored beans.

This guide splits by recipient type so you buy the right thing.

## What NOT to Gift

- **Flavored coffee beans** (unless you know they like them — most enthusiasts don't)
- **Novelty mugs** (they already have 15)
- **Coffee pod sampler packs** for a non-pod household
- **A $30 espresso machine** — it's a gift they'll throw away eventually
