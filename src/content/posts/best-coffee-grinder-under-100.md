## FAQ: Grinder Selection

**Q: What's the difference between conical and flat burrs?**
A: Conical burrs (nested) work well for all methods and are quieter. Flat burrs are faster and excel at filter coffee clarity. Both deliver excellent consistency vs. blade grinders.

**Q: Can I use a fine-grind espresso grinder for pour-over?**
A: Yes, but you'll sacrifice the grinder's strength. Espresso grinders shine at espresso's demanding settings; pour-over grinders are optimized for filter method convenience.

**Q: How often should I clean my burr grinder?**
A: Every 2-4 weeks, depending on bean oiliness. Oily dark roasts accumulate residue faster. Use a soft brush and a vacuum—never water.

**Q: Is "burr grinder" the same as "conical burr grinder"?**
A: No. "Burr grinder" is the category; conical and flat are two burr types. Both are far superior to blade grinders' inconsistency.

**Q: Can I grind for multiple brew methods with one grinder?**
A: Absolutely—the Breville, OXO, and Baratza excel here. If you primarily use one method, the Ode or Capresso might be more optimized.
