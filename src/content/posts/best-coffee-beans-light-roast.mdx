## Light Roast FAQ

**Are light roasts less caffeinated?** No — the caffeine-dark-roast myth persists but is essentially false. Roast level has minimal impact on caffeine content.

**Best brewing method?** Pour-over, AeroPress, and Chemex showcase light roasts best by controlling temperature and contact time precisely.

**Why does my light roast taste sour?** Under-extraction — water too cool, grind too coarse, or not enough contact time. Brew at 200–205°F.
