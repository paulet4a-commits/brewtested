## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I really need a dosing ring for manual grinding?**
A: If you're making espresso or need consistent doses for any brewing method, a dosing ring eliminates guesswork and saves money by reducing wasted beans. For casual French press, it's optional—but for serious coffee, it's essential.

**Q: Will a dosing ring fit my grinder?**
A: Most universal rings work with hand grinders from Comandante, 1Zpresso, Kingrinder, and others. Electric grinder rings are more model-specific. Check the listing for compatibility before purchasing.

**Q: How do I maintain my dosing ring?**
A: Wipe with a dry cloth after each use to remove coffee residue. Clean with a damp cloth weekly. Avoid dishwasher for magnetic versions. Store in a cool, dry place to prevent corrosion.

**Q: What's the difference between a dosing ring and a dosing funnel?**
A: A dosing ring controls the amount of beans loaded into the grinder. A dosing funnel funnels ground coffee directly into your portafilter. Some products combine both functions for maximum precision and reduced spillage.
