## What to Look For in a Mug Warmer

**Temperature range:** Ideal coffee drinking temperature is 130–145°F. Fixed-heat warmers often run at 120–130°F — acceptable but on the cooler end. Smart warmers let you dial in exact degrees.

**Auto-shutoff:** Any warmer you leave on a desk should have auto-shutoff. You will forget it. Get one with a timer (2–4 hours) unless you're always watching it.

**Mug compatibility:** Check your mug's base diameter. Most warmers fit mugs with a 2.5–3.5 inch base. Double-walled insulated tumblers work poorly — the insulation prevents heat transfer.

**Wattage:** Higher wattage (25W+) heats faster and maintains temperature better. Sub-15W warmers struggle with cold mugs.
