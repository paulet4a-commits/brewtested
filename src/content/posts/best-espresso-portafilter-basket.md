## Espresso Basket FAQs

**What size basket do I need?**
Standard espresso baskets come in single (7-8g) and double (18-20g) sizes. Some machines offer triple baskets (25-30g). Check your portafilter handle to see what size it holds. Most home machines use double baskets. The basket must fit snugly inside your portafilter—measure the diameter and depth before ordering.

**Will a precision basket fit my espresso machine?**
Most precision baskets (IMS, VST, Artisan) are universal and fit any standard 58mm portafilter. Some machines use 51mm or 54mm portafilters—measure your group head opening before buying. Check the Amazon product page for your specific machine compatibility, or contact the basket manufacturer.

**What's the difference between ridged and ridgeless?**
Ridged baskets have grooves that guide water distribution and increase contact time with the coffee puck. Ridgeless baskets rely on the puck itself to distribute water. Ridged = more forgiving of uneven tamping. Ridgeless = lower resistance, better for light roasts. Start with ridgeless if you're learning, switch to ridged for consistency.

**How often should I replace my portafilter basket?**
A quality basket lasts 5+ years with proper care. Replace when you notice permanent dents, coating damage, or visible wear on the ridges. Even damaged baskets work, but precision deteriorates. Budget replacement every 3-5 years depending on shot volume.
