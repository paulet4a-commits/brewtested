## Moka Pot FAQ

**Is a moka pot the same as espresso?**
No. Moka pots brew at 1–2 bars of pressure; espresso machines at 9 bars. The result is concentrated and strong but not true espresso — no crema, different extraction chemistry.

**What grind size for a moka pot?**
Medium-fine — coarser than espresso, finer than drip. Espresso-fine grind can clog the filter and over-pressurize.

**Aluminum vs. stainless — which tastes better?**
Stainless is flavor-neutral. New aluminum can impart a slight metallic taste until fully seasoned (10–15 brews). Seasoned aluminum tastes identical to stainless.
