## FAQ

**What size carafe do I actually need?**
For single coffee drinkers or couples, 8-cup machines are perfect and brew faster. Households of 3+ benefit from 10-12 cup capacity. Offices should go bigger. One "cup" in coffee terminology is about 5 ounces—a standard mug holds roughly 1.5-2 cups.

**Should I buy thermal carafe or glass?**
Thermal carafes keep coffee hot for 4+ hours without a heat plate, saving electricity. Glass carafes cool faster but let you see coffee level and taste less like metal. Thermal is better for busy households; glass is better for offices with constant brewing.

**Do expensive coffee makers really taste better?**
Temperature consistency and water distribution make measurable flavor differences. A $90 machine certified for optimal brewing will produce noticeably better coffee than a $40 machine—especially with specialty beans. For basic drip coffee, budget machines are acceptable.

**How often should I clean my coffee maker?**
Weekly rinse is essential; monthly deep clean with white vinegar removes mineral buildup. Hard water areas should clean every 2 weeks. Cleaning extends machine life and prevents flavor degradation.
