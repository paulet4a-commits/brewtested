## How Often to Descale

- **Hard water areas:** every 4–6 weeks
- **Soft water areas:** every 8–12 weeks
- **Warning sign:** brew time slows by 10–15 seconds

**Vinegar vs. descaler:** Vinegar works for light deposits but leaves persistent smell for weeks. Proper descalers rinse cleanly — worth the minimal cost.
