## FAQ

**Is a burr grinder really necessary?**
Yes, if you care about flavor. Blade grinders create inconsistent sizes — some grounds over-extract (bitter), others under-extract (weak). Burr grinders solve this completely.

**How long do burrs last?**
500–1,000+ hours of grinding — roughly 3–5 years of daily home use.

**Flat vs. conical burrs — which is better?**
Flat burrs produce slightly more uniform sizes. Conical burrs are quieter and easier to clean. Practical difference at this price range is minimal.
