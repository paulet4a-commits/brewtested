## Single Serve FAQ

**Are single serve coffee makers worth it?**
For solo drinkers or mixed-preference households, yes — you eliminate wasted coffee and get exactly what you want, when you want. The convenience premium is real.

**K-Cup vs. ground coffee — which is better?**
Ground coffee wins on quality and cost per cup. K-Cups win on convenience and variety. If you drink 500 cups/year, K-Cups cost $100–200 more than ground coffee.

**Do single serve machines make espresso?**
No. Single serve pod machines brew standard coffee at low pressure. For espresso, see our [best espresso machine under $100](/posts/best-espresso-machine-under-100/) guide.
