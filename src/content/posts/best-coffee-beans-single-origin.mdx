## Origins Guide: What to Expect

### Ethiopian Coffees
**Profile:** Floral, fruity, tea-like, bright acidity. Natural process = wilder berry notes.
**Best:** Light to medium roast. Pour-over.
**Buy:** Intelligentsia Black Cat or Volcanica Ethiopian Yirgacheffe.

### Kenyan Coffees
**Profile:** Wine-like, structured, complex acidity. Blackberry, red currant, stone fruit.
**Best:** Medium roast. Pour-over, espresso.
**Buy:** Blue Bottle Bella Donovan, Counter Culture Halo Nairobi.

## Single-Origin FAQ

**Single-origin vs. blend?** Single-origin = one country/farm/lot, shows geographic personality. Blends prioritize consistent house flavor. Single-origins teach you coffee diversity.

**Which origin for beginners?** Ethiopian or Colombian medium roast. Forgiving, approachable, clear flavor characteristics.

**Good price for single-origin?** $12–18/lb is specialty quality. Below $10 is usually blend territory. $25+/lb is premium micro-lot.

**Always buy whole bean.** Pre-ground loses aromatics within minutes. Whole beans stay fresh 2–4 weeks post-roast in an airtight container.
