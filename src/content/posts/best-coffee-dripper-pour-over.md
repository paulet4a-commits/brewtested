## FAQ

**What filter type do I need?**
Most drippers use standard cone or flat-bottom filters. Hario V60 filters have a unique spiral, Chemex uses proprietary thick paper, and Kalita Wave requires wave filters. Check your dripper's specifications before ordering.

**Can I use any coffee grind?**
Pour-overs work best with medium-fine grinds (think sand texture). Too coarse, and water passes through too quickly; too fine, and over-extraction creates bitterness. A burr grinder is essential for consistency.

**How long does brewing take?**
Most pour-over brews take 2.5 to 4 minutes from start to finish. The ritual is part of the appeal—it's a mindful morning practice rather than a push-button convenience.

**Are pour-overs better than automatic machines?**
Pour-overs offer more control and typically produce cleaner, brighter coffee. Automatics are more convenient. It depends on your priorities: taste and ritual versus speed and consistency. Many coffee lovers use both.
