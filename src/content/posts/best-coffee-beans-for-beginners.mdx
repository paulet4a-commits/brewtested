## Beginner FAQ

**Do I need a grinder?** Yes. Pre-ground stales within days. A $30–50 burr grinder makes a dramatic difference. Not essential day one, but upgrade soon.

**Light vs. dark roast?** Light = more origin flavor (fruity, tea-like). Dark = more roast flavor (chocolate, caramel). Both can be smooth.

**Whole bean vs. pre-ground?** Always whole bean. Ground fresh before brewing tastes dramatically better.
