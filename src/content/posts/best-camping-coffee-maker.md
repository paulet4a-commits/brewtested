## Camping Coffee FAQ

**What's the best method for backpacking coffee?**
AeroPress Go for quality + versatility. GSI JavaDrip for ultralight. Both produce excellent coffee without electricity or complex setup. Avoid pod-based systems (weight + waste).

**Do I need to bring pre-ground coffee camping?**
Pre-ground works — but whole beans stay fresher longer. For multi-day trips, pre-grind at home just before you leave and seal in an airtight bag. See our [best coffee storage](/posts/best-reusable-coffee-filter/) tips. For maximum freshness on long trips, a JavaPresse manual grinder from our [best coffee grinder under $50](/posts/best-coffee-grinder-under-50/) weighs 1 pound and packs small.

**How do I make coffee without electricity while camping?**
All five brewers on this list require only hot water, no electricity. Heat water with a camp stove, Jetboil, or over a fire to 195-205°F (just off a full boil), then brew as normal.

**Can I make espresso camping?**
Yes — with the Wacaco Nanopresso. It generates 18-bar pressure manually. Pack Nespresso capsules with the NS adapter for the easiest camp espresso experience.

**What coffee should I take camping?**
Dark-medium roast, pre-ground to your method. Pre-ground coffee will stay acceptably fresh for a 3-4 day trip in an airtight bag. For longer trips, grind fresh with a manual burr grinder.
