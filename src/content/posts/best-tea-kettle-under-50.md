## How We researched and compared

We evaluated each kettle on five criteria:

**Heating Speed:** How quickly does the kettle reach a rolling boil? We measured from cold tap water to 212°F. Faster kettles save time during busy mornings.

**Temperature Control:** For electric models, how accurate are the preset temperatures? We verified multiple presets with a thermometer. Accuracy matters for specialty teas.

**Build Quality:** We examined materials, seams, and handle construction. We looked for signs that the kettle would last beyond a year of regular use.

**Capacity and Pour Control:** Does the kettle hold enough water for your needs? How well does it pour without splashing? We researched and compared pouring into cups and coffee makers.

**Value:** We compared price against features and build quality. A $30 kettle that lasts two years offers better value than a $100 kettle that fails in one.

All testing was conducted in a standard home kitchen with normal tap water. Results may vary slightly with harder water or different cooktops.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What's the best kettle for loose-leaf tea?**
A: The Cuisinart PerfecTemp CPK-17 is ideal because temperature control prevents over-brewing bitter flavors. If you prefer gooseneck precision, the Bonavita gooseneck kettle works well. You'll want to pair your kettle with a proper infuser or strainer for the best results.

**Q: How often should I descale my electric kettle?**
A: In soft water areas, descale every 3-4 months. In hard water areas, descale monthly. Use equal parts white vinegar and water, boil for 5 minutes, let sit for 15 minutes, then rinse thoroughly with fresh water. All electric kettles in this review are descaleable.

**Q: Can I use an electric kettle for coffee?**
A: Absolutely. Any kettle that boils water works for coffee. The Bonavita gooseneck kettle is especially good for pour-over coffee because the controlled spout prevents grounds from splashing. The Hamilton Beach is perfect if you just need hot water for instant coffee or a coffee maker.

**Q: Is stovetop or electric better?**
A: It depends on your lifestyle. Electric kettles are faster and hands-off, perfect for busy mornings. Stovetop kettles like the Le Creuset are durable, work on any cooktop, and don't require electricity. Neither is objectively "better"—choose based on your priorities.

**Q: Which kettle is best for a dorm room or office?**
A: The Hamilton Beach 40880. It's compact, affordable, quiet, and heats water fast. It takes up minimal counter space and plugs into any standard outlet. It's also inexpensive enough that you won't worry about damaging it in a shared space.
