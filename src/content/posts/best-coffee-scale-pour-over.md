## FAQ: Pour Over Coffee Scales

**Q: Do I really need a coffee scale for pour over?**
A: Yes, if you want consistency. Scales remove guesswork from water-to-coffee ratios, making your coffee taste the same every time. Even basic scales dramatically improve brewing results compared to eyeballing measurements.

**Q: What's the minimum accuracy I need?**
A: For home pour over, ±1g accuracy is perfectly adequate. Premium ±0.1g scales are nice but not necessary unless you're chasing advanced brewing techniques or espresso.

**Q: Should I measure coffee beans before or after grinding?**
A: Weigh before grinding. This ensures consistency, since bean size and density vary between varieties. Grind, then measure your water by weight after the coffee grounds are in the dripper.

**Q: What water-to-coffee ratio should I use?**
A: The 1:16 ratio is an excellent starting point (1g coffee to 16g water). Some prefer 1:15 for stronger coffee or 1:17 for lighter brews. Your scale helps you dial in your preferred ratio precisely.

**Q: Is a timer function essential?**
A: Not essential, but helpful. You can use your phone's stopwatch, but built-in timers on scales keep you focused on pouring rather than checking another device.
