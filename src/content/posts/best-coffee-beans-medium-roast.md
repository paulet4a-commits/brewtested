## Medium Roast Coffee FAQs

**What's the difference between medium and dark roast?**
Medium roasts preserve more origin characteristics (fruitiness, floral notes) while adding caramelization and slight sweetness. Dark roasts emphasize bitterness and roasting notes, often masking the bean's origin. Medium roasts offer the best of both worlds.

**How should I store medium roast coffee beans?**
Keep beans in an airtight container away from light, heat, and moisture. Room temperature is fine. Avoid the freezer—moisture condensation degrades flavor. Use within 4 weeks of roast date for optimal freshness. If stored properly, beans stay fresh for up to 8 weeks.

**Can I use medium roast for espresso?**
Absolutely. Some medium roasts are specifically optimized for espresso (like Lavazza Gran Filtro). The key is using proper espresso technique—correct grind size, pressure, and tamping. Medium roasts in espresso will taste brighter than dark roasts but still deliver excellent body.

**Which medium roast is best for beginners?**
Start with Caribou Coffee Caribou Blend or Lavazza Gran Filtro. Both are forgiving, widely available, and affordable. They don't require expensive equipment or advanced technique to taste good. Once you're comfortable brewing, experiment with specialty roasts from Counter Culture or Dripkit.
