## Travel Mug FAQ

**How long should a travel mug keep coffee hot?**
Quality vacuum-insulated mugs keep coffee at drinkable temperature (above 140°F) for 4-6+ hours. Anything less than 3 hours is unacceptable for a quality mug.

**Are expensive travel mugs worth it?**
Yes — the temperature retention difference between a $15 mug and a $30 vacuum-insulated mug is dramatic. Budget non-insulated mugs keep coffee hot for 30-45 minutes. Quality vacuum-insulated mugs hold heat for 5-6 hours.

**What size travel mug should I get?**
- 12oz: Standard coffee shop drink size, more portable
- 16oz: Two average cups of coffee, fits most cup holders
- 20oz+: For long commutes or big coffee drinkers

**Can I put a travel mug in the dishwasher?**
Check your specific mug — most modern travel mugs have dishwasher-safe bodies and lids. The Contigo, YETI, and Hydro Flask on this list are all dishwasher safe.

**Do travel mugs affect coffee flavor?**
Stainless steel interior mugs (all picks on this list) are flavor-neutral. Plastic-lined mugs can impart a slight plastic taste, especially when new.
