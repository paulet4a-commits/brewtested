## Do You Need Variable Temperature?

- **Light roasts:** 200–205°F — higher temp extracts more from lighter roasts
- **Medium roasts:** 195–200°F — the standard sweet spot
- **Dark roasts:** 185–195°F — lower temp prevents bitterness

If you always brew one roast at one temperature, the Cosori fixed-temp is fine. If you brew across roast levels, variable temperature pays off.
