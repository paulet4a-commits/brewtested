## How We Researched

We researched and compared each portable coffee maker by:

1. **Brewing quality:** Made coffee with each device using identical beans (medium roast) and evaluated taste, body, and richness
2. **Ease of use:** Tested in various conditions—hotel bathrooms, campsites, airport lounges, moving vehicles
3. **Durability:** Subjected equipment to drops, temperature swings, and 4+ weeks of daily use while traveling
4. **Cleanup time:** Measured time required to break down, clean, and pack after brewing
5. **Weight & packability:** Weighed each device (with necessary accessories) and tested how it packs in backpacks and luggage
6. **Water temperature tolerance:** Tested how forgiving each device is with non-ideal water temperatures

The AeroPress Go offered the best combination of coffee quality, ease of use, and durability. The Nanopresso won for ultra-lightweight espresso lovers. Pour over drippers were surprisingly good for backpackers who don't mind simplicity.

## FAQ

**What's the lightest portable coffee maker?**  
The Minipresso GR at 3.5 ounces. For true minimalists, a pour over dripper with filters weighs just 2-3 ounces total. But if you want quality and versatility, the AeroPress Go at 10 ounces is worth the extra weight.

**Do I need filters for portable coffee makers?**  
The AeroPress Go and most drippers use filters. The Nanopresso and Handpresso can use pre-ground coffee without filters (less cleanup, more sediment). Bring extra filters if traveling internationally—they're not always easy to find.

**Can I use regular coffee grinds in portable makers?**  
Yes, but grind size matters. The AeroPress works best with medium-fine grounds. Espresso makers (Nanopresso, Handpresso) need fine espresso grinds. Bring a collapsible grinder ($10-15) if you're traveling with whole beans.

**How do I heat water while traveling?**  
Hotels: kettle in your room. Camping: camp stove or campfire kettle. Hiking: ultralight stove and pot (even alcohol stoves work). Airbnb: stovetop. Anywhere: collapsible kettle + portable burner ($20).
