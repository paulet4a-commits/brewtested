## Original vs Vertuo — The Core Difference

**OriginalLine:** 19-bar pump, traditional espresso pressure → concentrated 1–2oz shots with real crema.

**VertuoLine:** Centrifugal force, larger capsules → 1–14oz range (espresso to full mug). Not interchangeable with Original capsules.

Original = traditional espresso. Vertuo = flexibility and larger servings.

## FAQ

**Original vs Vertuo — which should I buy?**
Original if you want traditional espresso shots and the widest capsule variety. Vertuo if you want both espresso and regular-sized coffee from one machine.

**Do Nespresso capsules expire?**
They last 9–12 months from production. The machine works fine past the date but flavor fades.

**Is Nespresso cheaper than a coffee shop?**
Yes. Capsules run $0.40–0.80 per shot vs. $3–6 for a café espresso. Payback period is typically 2–4 months of daily use.
