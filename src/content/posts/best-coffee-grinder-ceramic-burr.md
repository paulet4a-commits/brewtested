## Ceramic Burr Grinder FAQ

**Are ceramic burrs better than steel burrs?**
Ceramic burrs generate less heat during grinding and produce slightly more consistent particle sizes. Steel burrs last longer and handle oily espresso beans better. For pour-over and French press, ceramic is superior. For espresso daily drinkers, steel edges out ceramic by 2-3%.

**How long do ceramic burrs last?**
High-quality ceramic burrs like Hario and Zassenhaus last 5-10 years with normal home use. Budget ceramic burrs may degrade after 2-3 years. Check warranty coverage before purchasing.

**Can ceramic burrs grind espresso-fine?**
Yes. Premium models like Zassenhaus Santiago excel at espresso-fine grinds. Budget ceramic grinders sometimes struggle producing espresso fineness—test before assuming capability.

**Do ceramic burrs produce more dust?**
No. Ceramic and steel burrs produce similar fines. The difference is minimal for home brewing. Both are superior to blade grinders in this regard.

**Should I hand-grind or buy electric ceramic grinder?**
Choose hand-grinding if you: brew 1-2 cups daily, value ritual, have limited budget, travel frequently, or live in small spaces. Choose electric ceramic if you: brew 3+ cups daily, have counter space, want timer convenience, or prefer speed.
