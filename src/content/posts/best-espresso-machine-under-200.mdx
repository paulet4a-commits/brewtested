## Espresso FAQ

**What pressure does espresso need?**
9 bars is the standard for espresso extraction. Higher pump ratings (15-bar, 20-bar) are maximum capability specs — what matters is consistent 9-bar pressure during extraction.

**Do I need a grinder?**
Yes, if using ground coffee (not pods). Espresso requires a very fine, consistent grind that pre-ground coffee can't achieve reliably. See our [best burr grinder under $100](/posts/best-burr-grinder-under-100/) for pairing recommendations.

**How long do budget espresso machines last?**
Budget machines ($100–200): 2–5 years with regular cleaning. Descaling every 2–3 months significantly extends lifespan.
