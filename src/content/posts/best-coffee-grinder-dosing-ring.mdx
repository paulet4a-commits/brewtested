## Dosing Ring vs. Dosing Funnel

**Dosing ring:** Controls the amount of beans loaded into the grinder. Sits above the burrs.

**Dosing funnel:** Channels ground coffee from grinder exit directly into your portafilter. Reduces bench scatter.

**Both combined:** The Weigh-and-Fold does both — dose control + funnel delivery in one accessory.
