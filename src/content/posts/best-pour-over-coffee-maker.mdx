## Pour Over Brewing Guide

**Ratio:** 1g coffee per 17g water. Practical: 1 tablespoon (~7g) per 120ml water.

**Water temp:** 195–205°F (90–96°C). Let boiling water rest 30 seconds.

**Method:**
1. Place filter, rinse with hot water (removes paper taste, preheats brewer)
2. Add grounds, create a slight well in center
3. Bloom: pour 2× coffee weight in water, wait 30–45 seconds
4. Main pour: slow circular pours over 2–3 minutes total
5. Let dripper finish draining naturally — don't squeeze

**Grind:** Medium (like sand). Adjust coarser if bitter, finer if sour.
