## Coffee Subscription FAQ

**Is a coffee subscription worth it?**
Yes — if you drink specialty coffee regularly. Grocery store beans are typically 3-6 months old. Subscription beans arrive 2-7 days post-roast. Fresh beans taste dramatically better. At $14-16/bag, most subscriptions are cheaper per cup than a café.

**How much coffee does a subscription bag make?**
A standard 12oz bag makes approximately 17-20 cups of coffee (using the standard 2 tablespoons per 6oz ratio). Frequency should match your drinking habits — 1 bag per week for heavy drinkers, 1 bag every 2-4 weeks for occasional coffee drinkers.

**Can I cancel a coffee subscription anytime?**
All services on this list offer cancellation at any time. Trade, Mistobox, and Volcanica are especially easy to pause or skip without cancelling entirely.

**What's the difference between specialty and regular coffee?**
Specialty coffee scores 80+ on the Specialty Coffee Association (SCA) 100-point scale. Regular grocery store coffee typically scores below 80. Specialty coffee has more nuanced flavor, better traceability, and is usually fresher.

**Should I get ground coffee or whole bean in a subscription?**
Whole bean — always. Ground coffee goes stale within 1-2 weeks. Whole beans stay fresh 2-4 weeks after opening. Grind immediately before brewing for best results. See our [best burr grinder under $100](/posts/best-burr-grinder-under-100/) for grinder recommendations.
