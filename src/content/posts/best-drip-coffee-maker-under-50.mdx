## Drip Coffee FAQ

**Which machine brews the best-tasting coffee?**
At this budget, taste depends more on your beans and grind than the machine. The Cuisinart (gold-tone filter) and Hamilton Beach produced the cleanest cups in our evaluation.

**How long do budget drip machines last?**
Expect 3–5 years of daily use with regular cleaning. Descale every 1–3 months depending on water hardness.
