## FAQ

**How many cups per person should I plan for?**
Assume 2–3 cups per coffee drinker per day. For 10 coffee drinkers, plan 20–30 cups/day minimum.

**Pod vs. drip for offices?**
Drip wins on cost per cup ($0.05–0.15 vs. $0.40–0.80 for pods). Pods win when variety and individual preferences matter more than cost.
