## Does Organic Taste Different?

Not necessarily better — but potentially cleaner. Organic farming prioritizes soil health, which can enhance flavor development. That said, roast level and origin matter more than organic certification alone for cup quality.

**Fair-trade vs. organic:** Different certifications. Fair-trade ensures minimum price to farmers. Organic means no synthetic chemicals. Best brands offer both.
