## FAQ

**Q: What's the best brewing method for two people?**
A: It depends on your lifestyle. For maximum flavor, choose AeroPress or Chemex. For convenience, choose Bonavita or Keurig K-Duo. For speed, choose Ninja. There's no single "best"—only best for your priorities.

**Q: Do I need a grinder?**
A: For AeroPress, Chemex, and Bonavita, yes. For Keurig K-Duo and Ninja with pre-ground coffee, no. See our [Best Coffee Grinder Under $50](/posts/best-coffee-grinder-under-50/) guide if you need one.

**Q: Can I brew larger batches for guests?**
A: Yes, but these are optimized for two. The Chemex (30oz) and Keurig K-Duo carafe (60oz) handle 4-6 cups. For regular entertaining, consider the [Best Coffee Maker Under $50](/posts/best-coffee-maker-under-50/) guide for larger machines.

**Q: What about water quality?**
A: Use filtered water in all these brewers. Chlorine and minerals affect flavor and can damage heating elements. A basic pitcher filter ($10-20) is recommended.

**Q: How often do these need cleaning?**
A: AeroPress: after each use (30 seconds). Chemex: after each use (2 minutes). Bonavita/Ninja: weekly rinse, monthly deep clean. Keurig: monthly descaling. All are manageable for two people.

**Q: Can I brew ahead for later?**
A: AeroPress and Chemex don't store well (tastes flat in 20 minutes). Bonavita carafe and Keurig thermal carafe stay hot 1-2 hours. If you need pre-brew capability, choose Keurig K-Duo.
