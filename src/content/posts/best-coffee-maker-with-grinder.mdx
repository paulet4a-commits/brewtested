## When a Grind-and-Brew Makes Sense

**Buy a grind-and-brew if:** You want fresh grounds without managing two devices, counter space is limited, and convenience matters more than dialing in precise extraction.

**Buy separate grinder + coffee maker if:** You care about extraction precision, grind consistency, or want to upgrade either device independently.

The Breville Grind Control is the only grind-and-brew here that approaches standalone grinder quality. Everything else is a convenience compromise.
