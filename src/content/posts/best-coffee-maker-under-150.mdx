## FAQ

**Do I need SCA certification?**
SCA (Specialty Coffee Association) certification means the machine brews between 196–205°F and has appropriate contact time. Both Bonavita and Moccamaster are certified. It's a reliable quality signal.

**Thermal vs. glass carafe — which is better?**
Thermal keeps coffee hot without a heating plate (which scorches coffee over time). Glass carafes are lighter and let you see coffee level, but degrade flavor faster on a hot plate.
