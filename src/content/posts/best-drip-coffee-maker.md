## How We Researched

We researched and compared each machine over 3 weeks with the same medium-roast beans, same grind size, and same water. We measured:

- **Brew temperature** — with a calibrated thermometer at the carafe
- **Brew time** — full pot timing
- **Extraction consistency** — TDS (total dissolved solids) measurements
- **Carafe heat retention** — temperature at 30, 60, 120 minutes
- **Ease of use** — programming, cleaning, pouring

## Drip Coffee FAQ

**What temperature should a drip coffee maker brew at?**
The SCA recommends 195-205°F (90-96°C). Machines that brew below 190°F will under-extract — producing flat, weak coffee. Only the Breville and OXO on this list are certified to hit this range consistently.

**How much coffee per cup in a drip machine?**
2 tablespoons per 6oz of water — the standard coffee ratio. Most machines measure "cups" at 6oz, not 8oz.

**Do I need a programmable coffee maker?**
If you want coffee ready when you wake up — yes. Almost all machines on this list are programmable. Set it the night before and wake to fresh coffee.

**How often should I clean my coffee maker?**
Descale every 1-3 months depending on water hardness. Run water-only cycles between descales. Hard water deposits reduce brew temperature accuracy over time.

**Glass vs thermal carafe — which is better?**
- **Glass + heating plate:** Keeps coffee warm indefinitely but continues cooking it — coffee gets bitter after 30 minutes.
- **Thermal carafe:** Keeps coffee warm 2-4 hours without cooking it — better flavor preservation.

For best flavor, a thermal carafe is superior. For convenience, glass is easier to clean.
