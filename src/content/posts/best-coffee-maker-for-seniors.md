## Frequently Asked Questions

### What's the simplest coffee maker for a senior with arthritis?

The Hamilton Beach Easy Access is designed specifically for this. The removable brew basket sits at chest height (no bending), and the two large buttons are easy to press even with limited hand strength. The Keurig K-Mini Plus is also good because you don't need to manipulate small water fills—just pop in a K-Cup and press one button.

### Can I program a coffee maker to brew while I'm still sleeping?

Yes, both the Mr. Coffee 12-Cup Programmable and Cuisinart DCC-1100 have 24-hour programmable timers. You set the time the night before, fill the water and grounds, and wake up to freshly brewed coffee. This is especially helpful for seniors with morning stiffness.

### Are these coffee makers safe to leave on?

All the models we reviewed have auto-shutoff features. The Hamilton Beach shuts off after 2 hours, the Keurig shuts off after 30 minutes, and the Cuisinart has a 2-hour auto-shutoff. You don't need to worry about leaving the machine on accidentally.

### Which coffee maker is easiest to clean?

The Keurig K-Mini Plus is the easiest—just toss the used K-Cup and rinse the cup. The Hamilton Beach Easy Access is second-best because all parts are removable and dishwasher-safe. Traditional drip makers require more rinsing but are also simple to maintain.

### How much coffee should a senior drink daily?

That's a medical question best discussed with a doctor, but most seniors enjoy 1-2 cups per day. All our recommendations provide this amount easily—even the single-serve Keurig or the 1-4 cup Cuisinart setting.
