## Why Your Grinder Matters More Than You Think

Blade grinders chop unevenly — powder and chunks in the same batch. Some particles over-extract (bitter). Others under-extract (sour). Together: muddy coffee.

Burr grinders crush beans between two surfaces, producing uniform particle size. Uniform grind = even extraction = better coffee. This is non-negotiable. **If you're buying a grinder, burr only.**
