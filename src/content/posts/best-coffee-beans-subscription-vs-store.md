## How We researched and compared Coffee Freshness and Flavor

We evaluated each option across five criteria:

1. **Freshness Testing:** Measured CO2 release (freshly roasted beans off-gas more) and compared flavor profiles at 1, 7, and 14 days from roast date.

2. **Brewing Consistency:** Tested each in AeroPress, pour-over, and espresso machines to evaluate performance across methods.

3. **Value Calculation:** Divided total monthly cost (including shipping) by total pounds received.

4. **Subscription Flexibility:** Assessed pause/skip options, pricing transparency, and cancellation policies.

5. **Flavor Complexity:** Cupped each batch and recorded tasting notes for acidity, body, sweetness, and aftertaste.

**Key Findings:** Trade Coffee and Atlas Coffee Club maintained peak freshness for 21+ days due to roast-to-order and quick shipping. Supermarket beans peaked at 7 days. Local roasters stayed fresh longest due to same-day roasting but showed highest variance in quality. For serious coffee drinkers, the freshness advantage of subscriptions justified the premium cost.

## FAQ: Coffee Beans Subscription vs Store-Bought

### Are subscription coffee beans fresher than store-bought?

Yes, if you choose roast-to-order subscriptions like Trade Coffee or same-day local roasters. Most supermarket beans are 2-4 weeks old before purchase. However, if you buy store-bought beans roasted within 1-2 days (check the roast date), they can match subscription freshness temporarily. The advantage of subscriptions is guaranteed freshness on arrival.

### How long do coffee beans stay fresh?

Peak freshness: 7-14 days after roasting. Acceptable quality: up to 30 days in sealed bags. Stale: 45+ days. Store beans in airtight containers away from light and heat. Freezing extends life to 3-4 months without quality loss.

### Is subscription coffee cheaper than store-bought?

Subscriptions cost $10-14 per pound delivered. Store-bought ranges $7-16 per pound. Supermarket beans are cheaper per pound but lower quality. Specialty store-bought (Peet's, local roasters) costs $10-16 per pound. Over 12 months, a subscription like Trade Coffee ($132/month) costs $1,584, while buying specialty store-bought ($12/pound, 6 pounds monthly) costs $864. Budget wins, but specialty quality favors subscriptions when factoring freshness premium.

### Which brewing method works best with subscription beans?

Single-origin subscription beans shine in pour-over, AeroPress, and Chemex because these methods highlight the bean's unique characteristics. Commercial blends work better in espresso machines and drip coffee makers. Trade Coffee and Atlas Coffee Club specifically recommend brewing methods with each bean, matching their single-origins to your preferred equipment.

### Can I switch between subscriptions?

Yes. Both Trade Coffee and Atlas Coffee Club allow pauses and cancellations anytime without penalty. However, switching costs money upfront for new subscriptions. Our recommendation: commit to one for 3 months to establish a baseline, then switch if you want variety.
