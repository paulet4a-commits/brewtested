## FAQ

**Do budget coffee makers brew worse coffee than expensive ones?**
Not necessarily. Under $30, the difference is usually features, capacity, and durability — not actual coffee quality. A $100 machine might last longer or brew more consistently, but the cup difference is subtle.

**How long do budget coffee makers last?**
Typically 3–5 years of daily use. Simpler machines (Proctor Silex) usually last longer because there are fewer components to fail.

**Is it worth upgrading if I already have one of these?**
Not unless it breaks or you hit a specific pain point. These machines do their job. Save the upgrade money for a better grinder — it has a bigger impact on coffee quality than the machine itself.
