## FAQ: Dark Roast Coffee Questions

### What's the difference between dark roast and medium roast?

Dark roast beans are roasted longer, developing deeper color and bolder flavors. Dark roasts have lower acidity, more body, and smokier, earthier notes. Medium roasts retain more origin flavor and have brighter acidity.

### Is dark roast coffee stronger or higher in caffeine?

Dark and medium roasts have similar caffeine content—roast level doesn't significantly change caffeine levels. However, dark roast's bold flavor and body can feel "stronger" in the cup.

### Can I use dark roast in a pour-over?

Yes, dark roast works beautifully in pour-over brewing. It delivers full flavor and body that shines with the slower extraction rate. Use slightly hotter water (195-205°F) and a medium grind.

### How long do dark roast beans stay fresh?

Dark roast beans are best used within 2-3 weeks of opening. Store in an airtight container away from light and heat. Whole beans last longer than pre-ground, which should be used within 1-2 weeks.

### Which dark roast is best for French press?

Death Wish and Peet's Major Dickason's are excellent for French press. The immersion brewing method brings out the bold, full-bodied character these beans offer. Use a coarse grind and 4-minute steep time.
