## FAQ

**Q: Do I actually need a scale for coffee brewing?**  
A: If you're chasing consistency, absolutely. Hand-scooping introduces 10-15% weight variance per dose. A $35 scale pays for itself in saved beans within weeks.

**Q: What's the difference between gooseneck and regular kettles?**  
A: Gooseneck spouts (like Hario) offer precision water placement, essential for pour-over. Boiling kettles work fine for French press or AeroPress.

**Q: Can I use these accessories with any brewing method?**  
A: The scale and canister work universally. The kettle is optimized for pour-over but functional for any stovetop brew method. The travel mug is transport-only.

**Q: Why does bean storage matter so much?**  
A: Oxidation is the primary enemy of coffee flavor. Vacuum canisters like Fellow Atmos slow oxidation by 60-70% compared to standard containers, keeping beans brighter for 2-3 weeks longer.
