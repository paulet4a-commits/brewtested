## What to Expect at This Price

**Water temperature:** Budget machines heat water to about 190°F — slightly below the ideal 195–205°F range. The difference in cup quality is real but subtle. If taste is your priority above $20, look at the [best drip coffee maker under $50](/posts/best-drip-coffee-maker-under-50/).

**Build quality:** Plastic construction throughout. Expect 2–4 years of daily use before something gives out. Not designed for a decade of daily brewing.

**No programmable timer:** Every machine on this list is manual start. If you want to wake up to fresh coffee, you need to spend at least $25–30. See our [best programmable coffee maker guide](/posts/best-coffee-maker-programmable/).

## The $20 Coffee Maker Trick

Cheap coffee makers can be improved significantly by:
1. **Using fresh beans and grinding them yourself** — a $25 hand grinder plus $20 machine beats a $100 drip machine with stale pre-ground coffee.
2. **Preheating your carafe** — rinse the carafe with hot water before brewing to reduce heat loss on contact.
3. **Using filtered water** — chlorine in tap water is a bigger flavor factor than the machine at this price.
