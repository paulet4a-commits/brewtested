## Flat vs. Conical Burrs

**Flat burrs:** Uniform particle size → consistent extraction → less dialing-in for espresso. Better for espresso-focused setups.

**Conical burrs:** More forgiving, work well for filter coffee. Slightly more particle size variation but good enough for pour-over and French press.

If espresso is your primary method, flat burrs are worth the premium.
