## How to Brew French Press (The Right Way)

Most people under-extract their French press. The fix:

**Ratio:** 1g coffee per 15ml water. For a 34oz press: 22g coffee, 330ml water.

**Grind:** Coarse — like sea salt. Finer grinds slip through the filter and make the cup muddy.

**Temperature:** 200°F (93°C) — just off boil. Let boiling water sit 30 seconds.

**Method:**
1. Preheat the press (hot water in, swirl, dump)
2. Add grounds
3. Bloom — pour 50ml water, wait 30 seconds
4. Fill to top, stir gently
5. Lid on (don't press yet) — steep 4 minutes
6. Press slowly and steadily — 30 seconds
7. Pour immediately — leaving it sitting steeps the grounds and turns bitter
