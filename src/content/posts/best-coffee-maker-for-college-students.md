## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use a coffee maker in a dorm room?**
Most colleges allow small appliances like coffee makers. Check your dorm handbook, but single-cup brewers are almost always permitted.

**Q: How much coffee should I buy?**
Plan for 0.75 oz of ground coffee per 6 oz cup. A 1 lb bag of ground coffee makes about 16 cups. Buy whole beans and grind fresh for better flavor and cost efficiency.

**Q: What if my dorm has limited outlet space?**
The AeroPress Go and Mueller French Press require no electricity. Alternatively, use a power strip with surge protection.

**Q: Should I buy whole beans or ground coffee?**
Ground coffee is easier for dorm life (no grinder needed), but whole beans stay fresh longer. A hand grinder is cheap (~$15) and takes 2 minutes.

**Q: How do I clean my coffee maker quickly?**
Rinse the carafe immediately after brewing. For drip makers, run water through the filter basket weekly. For French presses, disassemble and rinse each part thoroughly to prevent mineral buildup.
