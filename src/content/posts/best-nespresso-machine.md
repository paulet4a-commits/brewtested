## The Two Systems: Original vs Vertuo

Before diving into individual machines, understand the fundamental difference. **OriginalLine** uses 19-bar pump pressure to force hot water through a tightly-packed capsule, producing a concentrated espresso shot (1-2 oz) with traditional crema. **VertuoLine** uses centrifugal force and a larger capsule with wider holes, pulling water through faster to create a wider range of drinks (1-14 oz) from espresso to coffee.

Original = traditional espresso. Vertuo = versatility and larger servings. They don't share capsules.

## FAQ

### What's the difference between OriginalLine and VertuoLine?

OriginalLine (19-bar pump) creates concentrated espresso shots using pressure—more like traditional espresso machines. VertuoLine (centrifugal force) uses a wider capsule and faster water flow to create everything from espresso to full coffee. Original tastes more intense and concentrated; Vertuo tastes more like drip coffee but with espresso structure. They're incompatible—don't mix capsules.

### Which system has cheaper capsules?

OriginalLine capsules average $0.70-0.80 per capsule. VertuoLine capsules average $0.85-1.00. The difference is small (~$3-5 per month if you drink 5 shots daily), but it adds up.

### Can I use regular coffee grounds instead of capsules?

No. Nespresso machines are proprietary capsule-only systems. If you want to use grounds, look at our guide on [best espresso machines under $200](/posts/best-espresso-machine-under-200/).

### Do I need a milk frother if I don't buy Lattissima?

Only if you want lattes and cappuccinos. For straight espresso, no. If you do want milk drinks, the Lattissima has it built-in, or you can buy a [separate frother](/posts/best-milk-frother/) for $30-60.

### How long do these machines last?

Nespresso machines are designed for 5-7 years of regular use. We've seen units run 10+ years with proper descaling (every 3 months). The pump is the most wear-prone component, but capsule-fed systems are gentler on pumps than ground coffee machines.
