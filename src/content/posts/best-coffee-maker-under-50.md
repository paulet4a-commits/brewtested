## How We Researched

We brewed 3 cups per machine per day over 2 weeks using the same medium roast beans (ground fresh each time). We measured:

- **Brew temperature** — using a digital thermometer at the carafe
- **Brew time** — from power-on to full carafe
- **Taste** — blind tasting with a 3-person panel
- **Cleanup time** — how long to disassemble, rinse, and reassemble

All machines were purchased at retail price. No manufacturer samples accepted.

## Buyer's Guide: What to Look For

### Carafe size
- 1–2 people → 4–5 cup machine
- 2–4 people → 10–12 cup machine
- Office → 12–14 cup machine

### Brew temperature
Ideal is 195–205°F. Below 175°F and your coffee will taste weak and under-extracted. Most budget machines hit 185–195°F, which is acceptable.

### Programmable timer
If you want to wake up to fresh coffee, a programmable timer is essential. The Hamilton Beach 49350 is the only sub-$50 machine We researched and compared with a reliable one.

### Reusable filter vs. paper
Reusable (mesh) filters save money and reduce waste. Paper filters produce slightly cleaner coffee. Both are fine — it's a preference call.

## FAQ

**Can a $30 coffee maker taste as good as a $200 one?**

For drip coffee, yes — up to a point. The main advantages of expensive machines are precise temperature control, pre-infusion (bloom), and build quality. If you're brewing standard drip with pre-ground beans, the BLACK+DECKER produces coffee that's 80% as good as machines costing 5x more.

**How often should I clean a budget coffee maker?**

Run a descaling cycle every 1–3 months depending on your water hardness. For daily cleaning, just rinse the carafe and filter basket. Never leave coffee sitting in the carafe — it oxidizes quickly and tastes stale within 2 hours.

**Are budget coffee makers reliable?**

The brands on this list (BLACK+DECKER, Mr. Coffee, Hamilton Beach, Cuisinart) have long track records. Expect 3–5 years of use with regular cleaning. Avoid generic no-name brands at the same price point — failure rates are much higher.

**What's the best budget coffee maker for an office?**

The Hamilton Beach 49350 — it's the only one under $50 with a 24-hour programmable timer and 12-cup capacity. Set it to brew before the office opens.
