## FAQ

**Do I need a grinder?**
Not necessarily. Pre-ground coffee from a store works fine in all these machines. When you're ready to improve coffee quality, adding a burr grinder makes the biggest single difference.

**How do I clean a drip coffee maker?**
Monthly: run a cycle with equal parts white vinegar and water, then two cycles of plain water. Weekly: wash the carafe and filter basket with dish soap.

**What coffee should I buy as a beginner?**
Start with a medium roast from a grocery store brand. Once you have your process dialed in, try a local roaster or specialty subscription.
