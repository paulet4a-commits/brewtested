## FAQ: Beginner Coffee Questions

**Q: Do I need a special grinder, or can I use the store grinder?**
A: Store grinders work for drip coffee, but a decent home grinder ($30-50) improves flavor noticeably and saves money long-term. Not essential starting out, but worth upgrading to.

**Q: How long do coffee beans stay fresh?**
A: Whole beans stay fresh for 2-3 weeks after opening in an airtight container. Buy smaller bags more often rather than stockpiling.

**Q: What's the difference between light and dark roast?**
A: Light roasts have more origin flavor (fruity, tea-like), dark roasts have more roast flavor (caramel, chocolate, smoke). Both can be smooth—it's about personal preference.

**Q: Do I really need to buy whole beans?**
A: Yes. Pre-ground coffee stales quickly (within days). Whole beans ground fresh taste dramatically better and cost about the same.

**Q: Should I buy espresso beans or regular coffee beans?**
A: For beginners, buy "regular" beans (meant for drip/pour over). They're more forgiving. Espresso requires more technique and equipment.
