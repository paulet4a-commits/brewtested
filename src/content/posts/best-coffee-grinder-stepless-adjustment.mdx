## Stepless vs. Stepped: When It Matters

**Espresso**: Stepless is nearly mandatory. Micro-adjustments between shots are essential — stepped grinders jump past your ideal setting.

**Filter coffee** (pour-over, drip, French press): Quality stepped grinder works perfectly. Stepless adds flexibility but isn't required.

**Rule**: If you pull espresso → go stepless. If you brew filter only → stepped is fine.
