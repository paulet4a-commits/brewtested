## FAQ

**What's the difference between a frother and a steamer?**
A steam wand injects steam directly into milk → true microfoam. An electric frother spins air in with a disc. Steam wands produce finer foam; frothers are more accessible.

**Which milk froths best?**
Whole milk easiest. Oat milk works well (use "barista edition"). Almond milk hardest — low protein resists frothing.

**Optimal milk temperature?**
140–160°F. Above 170°F, proteins denature and flavor goes flat.
